## Why this draft passes

| It does this | Which rule it satisfies |
|---|---|
| Leads with the organization's mission, not a feature list | Nonprofit voice: lead with community outcomes, not product features |
| Says "members" and "branch" | "Users" is banned; "gym" is banned in nonprofit content |
| Writes "Daxko Engage" in full | Full branded product name on first use |
| Makes no numeric claim | Nothing to source, so nothing to fail on claims and proof |
| One specific CTA with a time commitment | One clear CTA per piece; "learn more" is banned as generic |
| No urgency language | Aggressive urgency is an outright ban in the nonprofit market |
| Credits the customer's staff | Never position Daxko as the hero of customer outcomes |
