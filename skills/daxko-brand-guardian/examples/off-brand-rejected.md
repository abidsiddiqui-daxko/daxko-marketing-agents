# Example — content that fails

This sets the quality bar for a **REJECTED** review. Every violation quotes the draft, cites the
rule, and names the file the rule came from. No violation rests on taste.

---

## The submitted draft

> **Market:** club · **Type:** ad
>
> STOP LOSING MONEY!!! Our revolutionary app CRUSHES the competition and GUARANTEES 300% ROI in 30
> days or less. Every gym that doesn't use us is falling behind. Users love our seamless,
> best-in-class platform. Act now — last chance!
>
> Learn more.

---

## The review

**Reviewing:** ad · **Market:** club (stated)

## Verdict: REJECTED

## Scorecard

| Dimension | Result | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Voice and tone | fail | ALL CAPS, stacked exclamation marks, hype language, aggressive urgency |
| Terminology and naming | fail | Five banned words; "users" instead of members; no product named at all |
| Visual identity | pass | Text only, no visual elements to assess |
| Audience fit | fail | Shouts at club operators instead of speaking peer-to-peer with metrics |
| Claims and proof | fail | "GUARANTEES 300% ROI in 30 days" is unverifiable and unsourced |
| Call-to-action clarity | fail | "Learn more" is a banned generic CTA, stacked with two urgency tactics |

## Violations

**1. Unverifiable, guaranteed ROI claim — the most serious problem here**
- **Quoted:** "GUARANTEES 300% ROI in 30 days or less"
- **Rule:** "Never make unsubstantiated ROI claims." "Use real metrics with source attribution for all claims." — `references/brand-guidelines.md`
- **Why it matters:** This is an automatic fail on claims and proof. It is a promise Daxko cannot stand behind, and a guarantee of a specific return in a specific window is a commitment, not marketing language. This alone rejects the piece.

**2. Banned vocabulary — five instances**
- **Quoted:** "revolutionary", "seamless", "best-in-class", "users", "Learn more"
- **Rule:** All five are on the banned list. Approved replacements: revolutionary → new, purpose-built, modern · seamless → smooth, connected, integrated · best-in-class → *(state the specific advantage instead)* · users → members · learn more → "See It in Action", "Get the Guide", "Talk to Our Team" — `references/banned-words.md`
- **Why it matters:** "Users" is the one that will be noticed by customers. Daxko's language is members, students or clients.

**3. Banned constructions — ALL CAPS and stacked exclamation marks**
- **Quoted:** "STOP LOSING MONEY!!!", "CRUSHES"
- **Rule:** "ALL CAPS for emphasis (use bold or sentence structure instead)." "Stacked exclamation marks (one is acceptable sparingly; three is excessive; never stack)." — `references/banned-words.md`
- **Why it matters:** Club operators are experienced buyers. Shouting reads as a company with nothing specific to say.

**4. Aggressive urgency tactics**
- **Quoted:** "Act now — last chance!"
- **Rule:** "Never use aggressive urgency tactics ('Last chance!' / 'Act now!')." — `references/brand-guidelines.md`
- **Why it matters:** Both banned phrases appear, in one line.

**5. Attacking the competition, and no product named**
- **Quoted:** "CRUSHES the competition", "Every gym that doesn't use us is falling behind"
- **Rule:** "Reference competitor names negatively — compare on merits only." Competitive content must be "factual and defensible… never attack competitors directly. Avoid vague superlatives." And: use the full branded product name on first use — `references/brand-guidelines.md`
- **Why it matters:** The ad attacks unnamed competitors and never says which Daxko product it is selling.

**6. Wrong voice for the club market**
- **Quoted:** the whole piece
- **Rule:** Club voice is "direct, peer-to-peer with operators, not corporate", leading with "ROI and measurable outcomes (revenue, retention, payment recovery)". Banned: "generic operational claims without metrics" — `references/brand-guidelines.md`, `references/club-playbook.md`
- **Why it matters:** The draft has the volume of a metrics-led ad with none of the metrics.

## Suggested edits

| Line | Currently | Change to |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | "STOP LOSING MONEY!!!" | "Recover more of the revenue you have already earned" |
| Body | "Our revolutionary app CRUSHES the competition" | "Club Automation's payment recovery is built for multi-location operators" |
| Claim | "GUARANTEES 300% ROI in 30 days or less" | Use a sourced figure or remove it. `references/brand-guidelines.md` cites "Recover an average of 69% of payments with Daxko's 30 years of experience in revenue recovery" as a correctly-framed claim |
| Body | "Every gym that doesn't use us is falling behind" | Delete. Compare on merits or not at all |
| Body | "Users love our seamless, best-in-class platform" | Delete, or replace with a specific outcome and its source |
| CTA | "Act now — last chance! Learn more." | "See it in action — book a 20-minute demo" |

## Corrected rewrite

> **Recover more of the revenue you have already earned**
>
> Failed payments quietly cost clubs thousands every month. Club Automation recovers an average of
> 69% of them, built on 30 years of revenue recovery experience — no chasing, no awkward calls from
> your front desk team.
>
> See it in action — book a 20-minute demo.

*(Note: the 69% figure is sourced from `references/brand-guidelines.md`. Any figure that replaces it
must carry its own source, or the rewrite fails the same dimension the original did.)*

## What this example is demonstrating

| | |
|---|---|
| **Every violation cites a rule and names its file** | Six violations, six citations. Nothing rests on "this feels wrong" |
| **The draft is quoted exactly** | The reviewer can be checked; the writer can find the text |
| **Visual identity still says `pass`** | All six rows always appear. A text-only ad has nothing visual to fail |
| **One fail is enough** | Four dimensions failed here, but a single unsourced ROI claim would have rejected it on its own |
| **The rewrite repairs, it does not reinvent** | Same offer, same audience, same channel — fixed. Creating new content belongs to Agent 13 — Content Production |
| **The rewrite's own claim is sourced** | And the note says where from. A rewrite that introduces a fresh unsourced number repeats the original sin |
