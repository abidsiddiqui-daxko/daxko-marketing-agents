# Agent 37 — Social Media — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-client-social` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.8.0

## What it does
Plans and writes social posts for ONE Digital Services client: a calendar (dates, times, platforms), the text for each platform, image notes, what the client must approve, and a list ready to load into their scheduling tool. It asks short rounds of questions (up to 3 at a time) and uses only the client facts you paste.

## Requests to try
- "Social calendar for our YMCA client for March"
- "Write this club client's Instagram and Facebook posts for summer camp"
- "Four weeks of LinkedIn and Facebook posts for the JCC client's open house"

## What it may change, and only after your yes
If you have a connector to the scheduling tool, it can load the posts as **unscheduled drafts** (no publish time). It first shows you the exact list and waits for your "yes" in the chat. You become the owner of that change.

## What it never does
Schedule or publish a post, reply to comments or messages, delete anything, or boost a post. Someone schedules after the client approves.
