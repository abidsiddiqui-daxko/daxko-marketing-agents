- **Key personas:** Executive Director/CEO · Board of Directors · Development/Fundraising Director
- **Buying dynamic:** Lean/centralized, board ROI scrutiny, BGCA national office influences data standards, grant cycle timing
- **Campaign theme (confirmed NP-04/05):** "Ready Now for Club Growth" — movement-centered, NOT migration-centered, NOT competitive displacement

## AI Positioning for NP

**Core narrative:** Daxko is embedding AI directly into the operational systems customers already trust. This is not experimentation — it is reducing manual work, improving consistency, and strengthening engagement outcomes. AI at Daxko is becoming part of how the platform operates, not a separate tool teams must learn, manage, or secure on their own.

**The market problem we solve:** The market is flooded with standalone AI point solutions that create data silos and increase complexity.

**Security & trust message:** Daxko's integrated approach is more secure than disconnected tools. Data is not used to train public models. AI respects permissions. Customers control adoption.

### Always / Never for AI Content

**ALWAYS emphasise:** Trust, Security, and Choice · Operational Relief · Mission Alignment.

**NEVER say:** Aggressive replacement narratives · "One AI tool to rule them all" · Anything that minimises or dismisses partner value.

### NP-Specific Hard Rules (in addition to the above)

1. NEVER imply AI reduces headcount, staffing, or funding
2. NEVER suggest member data could be shared, aggregated across organizations, or used beyond the customer's own environment
3. NEVER position AI as a bolt-on or add-on product — always embedded
4. NEVER use technical AI language (LLMs, machine learning, neural networks)
5. NEVER imply AI is experimental or unproven — anchor to delivered capabilities
6. ALWAYS tie AI outcomes to mission language: community impact, member retention, operational clarity, board-trusted reporting

## Campaign Strategy Guardrails

### Always
1. Lead with mission impact, not features
2. Use NP language — members not customers, community not market
3. Reference peer organizations and industry benchmarks
4. Respect budget constraints — emphasize ROI and efficiency
5. Build around NP calendar cycles
6. Frame Daxko as a long-term partner enabling mission, not a vendor selling software
7. Use specific proof points from approved case studies
8. Map every campaign to a KR

### Never
- Aggressive urgency tactics ("limited time," "act now," "don't miss out")
- Lead with features or product specs
- "Sell," "close the deal," or "revenue" in prospect-facing content
- Reference customer stories without org approval
- Campaign without KR mapping
- "Impact," "accelerate," "gym," "[x], together"
- "$20.9M" pipeline target
- "Only purpose-built for BGCs" (no longer valid — MyClubHub)
- AI framing that implies headcount reduction or technical complexity
