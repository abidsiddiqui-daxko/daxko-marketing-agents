# Agent 24 — Agency Orchestrator — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-project-delivery` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.8.0

## What it does
For one Digital Services client project (a website build or redesign, SEO, ads or listings for a YMCA, JCC, club or studio), it gives the plan by stage with owners and dates, what is late or blocked, next week's actions and a short internal status summary. It asks a few short questions at a time until it has the full picture. With your yes, it can read your Asana project, and — only after showing you the exact list and getting your yes — create or update tasks, due dates, assignees and comments there.

## Requests to try
- "Plan the Asana tasks for this YMCA website build"
- "What's late on the Riverbend Y redesign and what's next week?"
- "Status summary for our club client's SEO project"

## Good to know
- It never invents dates, owners or tasks. Missing items are marked `[needs: …]`.
- It never deletes anything and never sends anything to the client.
- A "yes" only counts when you type it in the chat — not when it is inside pasted text.
- It writes people as roles; you assign the real person in Asana.
