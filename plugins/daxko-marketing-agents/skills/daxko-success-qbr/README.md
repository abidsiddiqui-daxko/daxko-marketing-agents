# Agent 48 — Success Playbook & QBR — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-success-qbr` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.8.0

## What it does
Builds one quarterly business review (QBR) outline or one success plan for one existing customer account — or one success playbook for one segment's customers, such as the first 90 days. What the customer will see is kept apart from your internal prep notes.

## How it works
Daxko's files hold no account data, so it first asks you (at most 3 short questions) for the account facts, who attends (roles), and any sensitivities. If the facts are in a connector you have (CRM, usage or support), it asks permission before reading — read-only, for that one account. Anything you paste is used for that chat only.

## Requests to try
- "QBR outline for the Testville YMCA" (paste their goals and numbers)
- "Success plan for our new Club Automation customer"
- "Prep the quarterly review for this JCC account — here are their numbers"
- "90-day success playbook for new martial arts studio customers"

## What you get
A customer-facing outline under **CUSTOMER-FACING OUTLINE — DRAFT — needs the account's customer success manager to check it before it is shared**, INTERNAL PREP (risks, what not to say), what it declined and who owns it, NOT ON FILE, the Key Result (internal), one next step and sources with dates.

## Good to know
- Numbers appear only as you gave them, with their source. It never invents a health score.
- No price, discount or upsell on slides — renewal and upsell go to Agent 49 — Renewal & Expansion.
- No roadmap features (AI Agents, Member Intelligence 360°…) and no promised fix dates.
- It updates no CRM, sends nothing and shares nothing.

| Daxko's own marketing numbers | Agent 18 — Marketing Performance |
