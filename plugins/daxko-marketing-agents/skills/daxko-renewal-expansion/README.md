# Agent 49 — Renewal & Expansion — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-renewal-expansion` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.7.35

## What it does
Helps an account manager with **one existing Daxko customer**: which Daxko product or add-on fits next and why, and how to prepare the renewal conversation. It answers from Daxko's own files plus the notes you paste about that one account. It can also map which add-ons fit a whole segment.

## Requests to try
- "Expansion plan for this YMCA" (paste a few notes: products they have, goals, issues)
- "Prep me for the renewal with our Club Automation customer" (paste the notes)
- "What can we upsell to our JCC customers?"
- "Help me talk to a V1 Club Automation customer about V2"

## What you get
Up to three ranked suggestions, each showing the Daxko file line it rests on and the fact from your notes it matches — or "fit not shown — ask first" with the question to ask. A renewal plan, a two-column talk track (internal fact | what you may say), approved proof, a NOT ON FILE list, the Key Result it serves, and sources with dates.

## Good to know
- Paste notes for **one account at a time**, without personal contact details. It refuses bulk CRM, billing or usage exports.
- It never invents usage, health scores, prices or discounts, and never promises a date or a feature to save a renewal.
- It never offers roadmap AI (Member Intelligence 360°, AI Agents, Intelligent Scoring) as an upsell, not even as "coming".
- Boys & Girls Clubs on Vision: no pressure to migrate. Club V1 → V2: what V2 unlocks, no promised date.
- It writes, sends and updates nothing. Who approves a discount and who signs off are not on file: confirm with your leader.
