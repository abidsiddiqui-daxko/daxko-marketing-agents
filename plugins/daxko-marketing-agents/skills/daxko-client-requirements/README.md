# Agent 25 — Onboarding & Requirements — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-client-requirements` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.10.1

## What it does
Helps a Digital Services project lead start one new client project — a website build or redesign, SEO, paid ads or listings. It writes the kickoff questions (or a kickoff agenda), turns the client's intake answers or kickoff notes into a clear requirements document — goals, audience, scope in and out, deliverables, pages named, integrations, what the client still owes, dates as given, open questions and risks — or checks a brief you paste for gaps. Daxko's files hold no Digital Services intake process or client facts, so it asks you up to 3 short questions first when something is missing.

## Requests to try
- "Kickoff questions for a new JCC website client"
- "Turn these intake answers into a project brief" (paste them)
- "Write the requirements doc from this kickoff call" (paste the notes)
- "What's missing from this project brief?" (paste it)

## What you get
The questionnaire, requirements doc or gap table under **DRAFT — needs the project lead before it goes out**, kept apart from internal notes, then what it declined and who owns it, a NOT ON FILE list, the nearest Key Result and sources with dates.

## Good to know
- Every line in a brief comes from what you pasted, marked "as pasted". Anything missing becomes `[needs: …]` and an open question — it never invents scope, pages, dates, budgets or prices.
- If your notes contradict themselves (a 4-week launch and 40 pages of new copy), it flags the risk instead of picking a side.
- It can pull the intake form or notes from your connector (Google Drive, Asana, Airtable) — only after asking you, read-only, for that task.
- It never asks for or repeats logins, passwords or card details: share access the secure way your project lead sets up. Client people appear as roles.
- It sends nothing and creates no tasks. Who signs off is not on file — confirm it.
