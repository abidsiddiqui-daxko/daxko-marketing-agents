# Agent 39 — CRO — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-site-conversion` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.8.0

## What it does
CRO = getting more website visitors to do the thing that matters (book a tour, join, send a form). You paste one site's or funnel's numbers for one period — Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity notes, or simple step counts — or it reads them from a connector you already have after you say yes. It checks they add up, works out where people drop out, and recommends changes in order, each tied to one of your numbers. Plus a short summary for the client.

## Requests to try
- "Our YMCA client gets 9,000 visits but only 40 tour bookings — why?"
- "Where are people dropping out of this JCC's join funnel?"
- "Clarity shows rage clicks on the membership page"

## Good to know
- It never changes the website and never starts or judges a test.
- No "average conversion rate" or promised lift unless you paste one.
- Clarity recordings and form entries: it works from counts only, never names or emails.
