# Agent 20 — Battlecard & Objection Handling — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-battlecards` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.7.35 · INTERNAL tool for sales reps

## What it does
Gives you a battle card for one competitor in one market, or a short, call-ready answer to an objection, using only Daxko's own competitive files. It shows where we lose as well as where we win. It marks anything it can't find as "not on file".

## Requests to try
- "Battle card for Amilia for a JCC call"
- "How do I answer 'ABC is cheaper'?"
- "How do we beat PushPress with CrossFit boxes?"
- "Objection sheet for Boys & Girls Clubs"
- Paste a prospect's claim: "they said X, is that true and how do I answer?"

## What you get
A card or answer labelled INTERNAL; trap questions from the files; an honest "where we lose"; a "what you may say to customers" column; a NOT ON FILE list; sources with dates.

## Good to know
- Paste call notes if you like, but remove names, emails and phone numbers first. It never repeats them.
- It never invents prices, win rates, customers or quotes, and never offers roadmap AI (AI Agents, Member Intelligence 360°) as available.
- ClassPass is a partner and Exercise.com is a Daxko product, so there is no battle card against either.
- Who signs off a card before reps use it is not on file: confirm who signs off.
