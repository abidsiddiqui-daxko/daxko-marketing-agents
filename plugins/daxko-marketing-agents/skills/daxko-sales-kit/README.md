# Agent 21 — Sales Enablement — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-sales-kit` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.7.35

## What it does
Drafts sales materials for one market and segment from Daxko's own files: a one-page leave-behind, a slide-by-slide deck outline, a discovery-call guide, a demo talk track or a buyer card. It uses approved proof, products and buyer pains only, and says "not on file" when the files are silent.

## Requests to try
- "One-pager for YMCA directors"
- "Outline a deck for a multi-location club"
- "Discovery questions for a JCC call"
- "Demo talk track for Zen Planner"
- "Buyer card for a martial arts studio owner"

## What you get
The draft, with anything a customer will read kept apart from internal notes; sources with dates; a NOT ON FILE list. Customer copy needs Agent 9 — Brand Guardian before use.

## Good to know
- It never invents ROI numbers, prices, customers or quotes. No exact prices: "contact sales for a quote". No Zen Planner free trial.
- It never offers roadmap AI (AI Agents, Member Intelligence 360°) as available, not even as "coming".
- Paste rep notes if you like, but remove names, emails and phones first.
- Who signs off a material before use is not on file: confirm who signs off.
