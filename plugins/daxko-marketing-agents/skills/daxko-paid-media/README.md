# Daxko Paid Media — Agent 17 — Paid Media

**Version:** 1.7.36 · **Skill:** `daxko-paid-media` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Type:** read-only data analyst

## What it does, in plain words
Paste last month's results from Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads or Meta for Daxko's own ads (Daxko, Club Automation, Zen Planner, Exercise.com). It tells you what worked, what didn't, and what a person could pause, scale or test — with every sum shown.

## What to give it
Results by campaign with dates, spend, impressions, clicks and conversions; the brand and period; what counts as a conversion; any target.

You can paste the numbers, or let it pull them through a connector you already have — it always asks first
("May I pull … read-only, for this task only?") and only goes ahead after you say yes.

## What it will never do
- Change anything in a live system (no campaign, ad, budget, bid, audience, schedule or status in Google Ads, LinkedIn, Meta or any ad tool — no pausing, no money moved).
- Invent a number, target or "industry benchmark".
- Repeat personal details (names, emails, phone numbers) — it leaves them out.
- Say a roadmap AI feature is live.
- Watch numbers over time or send alerts — it only runs when you ask.

## Example asks
- "How did our LinkedIn ads do last month?"
- "Which Google campaign is wasting money?"
- "Read this Meta export for Zen Planner."

## Who handles what instead
A client's ads → Agent 71 — Budget Reallocation · new ad copy → `daxko-ad-creative` · strategy before results → `daxko-paid-ads` · Key Result roll-up → Agent 18 — Marketing Performance.
