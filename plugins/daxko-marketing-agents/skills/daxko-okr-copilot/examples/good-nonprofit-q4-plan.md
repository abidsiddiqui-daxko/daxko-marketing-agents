### Sizing — the remaining pipeline gap, with the working shown

Sized against Agent 18 — Marketing Performance's gap figures as it gave them — never against an actual:

- **New Logo:** $3,624,382 (GAP — Agent 18 — Marketing Performance, measured 30 Sep 2026, `SAMPLE`) ÷
  $79,500 average new-logo deal size (ASSUMPTION — `nonprofit-playbook.md`, *Deal Economics*) = **45.59**
  average-size opportunities carrying pipeline value. 45.59 ÷ 42% of opportunities carrying pipeline value
  (ASSUMPTION, same table) = **108.5** new-logo opportunities created.
- **Expansion:** $9,794,649 (GAP — same reading, `SAMPLE`) ÷ $13,000 (ASSUMPTION) = **753.43**; 753.43 ÷
  75% (ASSUMPTION) = **1,004.6** expansion opportunities created.
- **The file's assumptions agree with its own targets:** $9,724,382 (TARGET) ÷ $79,500 = 122.3, against the
  file's 291 opportunities × 42% = 122.2; $16,194,649 (TARGET) ÷ $13,000 = 1,245.7, against 1,661 × 75% =
  1,245.8.

**What this sizing is not.** It is **not a Q4 target** — whether the team commits to closing all of the
remaining gap in Q4 is a decision: `[QUARTERLY TARGET — not defined]`. It is **not a forecast** — the win
rates in the same table (17% / 40%) are not applied, because turning pipeline into expected bookings is a
forecast. And it says **nothing about what any one item will add** — `[IMPACT — not estimated]`.

## Six things this example is teaching

1. **`cannot say` survives untouched.** Agent 18 — Marketing Performance's word is quoted with its date and
   reason, and nothing on the page — not the title, not an item's name — turns it into "behind" or "off
   track".
2. **A remaining gap is sized, not sliced.** The sums run on `GAP` and `ASSUMPTION` inputs, every step
   shown — and the page says in plain words that the result is not a Q4 target, not a forecast and not an
   impact. The win rates sit in the same table and are deliberately left unused.
3. **Live traps were walked past.** The BGC playbook's own *"~198 net new customers (from 31 to 229)"* and
   the nonprofit playbook's *"10% reactivation rate ($1.1M ARR)"* are in files this plan opened. Neither
   appears: one is a gap from an Aug 2025 actual, the other is an impact estimate.
4. **Delivery status is honoured to the letter.** Engage Pro AI is planned as sellable; the Q3 2026
   capabilities carry a placeholder; the Early 2027 capabilities are named only to say they are not in the
   plan.
5. **Every agent is named by number and name, and only the live ones as able to help.** Agent 63 —
   Non-Profit Campaign & Content Strategist is named as not yet available; nothing gets a date.
6. **One next step.** Item 1 is the first item that needs copy, so the offer is Agent 13 — Content
   Production. Item 2's whitepaper names Agent 11 — Thought Leadership & Long-Form in its row and is **not**
   offered — that would be a second offer.
