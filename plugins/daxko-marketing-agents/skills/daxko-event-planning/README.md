# Agent 23 — Event & Webinar (event planning) — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-event-planning` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.9.0
**Sister skill:** `daxko-event-promotion` (Agent 23 — Event & Webinar, event messaging) writes all the promotion copy.

## What it does
For ONE Daxko event — a trade show, conference booth or webinar — it runs Field Marketing's planning from contract signing to the recap. It reminds you to rename and pin the chat, asks for the 17 event details in short rounds (up to 3 questions at a time), works out the tier (Tier 1 = budget of $60k or more, or 30+ people) and builds each phase:

| Phase | What you get |
|---|---|
| 1 — contract signed | Event brief · Slack channel setup and pinned message · team selection message · travel emails |
| 2 — team and agenda known | Workback schedule · kickoff deck outline (20 slides Tier 1, 8 slides Tier 2) |
| 3 — 2–4 weeks out | 4 Slack canvases · 3 week-of Slack messages (Tier 2) |
| Any time | Organizer deliverables checklist + drafts · event-day field guide |
| Webinar | Roles, platform checklist, rehearsal, run of show, webinar workback |
| After | Post-event recap and the Event ROI score out of 25 |

## Requests to try
- "We just signed for IHRSA — set it up"
- "Workback schedule for our JCC conference booth on 14 September"
- "Organizer requirements pasted below — what's due when?"
- "Run of show for our Club Automation webinar on the 3rd"
- "Here are the Popl numbers and team notes — score our MAIACON event ROI"

## What it may change, and only after your yes
If you have a connector: create **new** Asana tasks for the workback or follow-ups; post the pinned or a week-of message in an **internal** Slack channel you name; save the brief or field guide as a document. It shows you the exact list first and waits for your "yes" in the chat. You become the owner of that change.

## What it never does
Send any email, book flights or hotels, order booth, swag or attire, submit to an organizer, post outside Daxko, delete anything or spend money. It asks you for shipping and return details each time and never repeats account numbers, passport numbers or home addresses.
