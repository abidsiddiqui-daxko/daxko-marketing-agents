## 8. WEEK-OF SLACK MESSAGES — Tier 2 only

Three messages, scheduled by milestone, not calendar date. **Tier 1:** do not generate — remind the person these are written one-off for each Tier 1 event, closer to the event, when full logistics are confirmed. With a connector and a yes, ONE message at a time may be posted in the internal event channel (CHANGE LIST) — this skill never schedules them.

**Message 1 — morning of setup / day 1**
```
@channel Good luck this week! Don't forget to take lots of pictures! 📸

Setup today is [time frame].

Tracking numbers (everything will be at the hotel under [name as given]'s name):
• [Item]: [tracking number]
• [Item]: [tracking number]

Reminder to use Popl and mark lead status correctly:
• **MQL** = quality conversation / you will follow up
• **Visited Booth** = they just stopped by / no opportunity
```

**Message 2 — morning of breakdown day**
```
Hey @channel! Here's the shipping info. Everything needs to be packed, labelled and shipped back:

**Booth:** the banner and tablecloth go back to the booth company. There should be a shipping label in the box they came in; if not, the return address is: [needs: return address and attention line — event manager]

**Swag:** all remaining swag goes back to our Sendoso warehouse. There should be a return label in the swag box; if not, use the ship notice [event manager attaches it here, with the shipping account added directly].

The ship notice goes on the outside of the box, barcode facing out. Several swag boxes → print copies and mark them box 1 of 2, 2 of 2, etc.

[Attach the ship notice to this message]
```

**Message 3 — 1 day after the event ends**
```
@channel Hey! Please drop a quick paragraph recap in this thread today with your key takeaways:

• Key customer conversations (wins, risks, expansion signals)
• Brand sentiment / talk about competitors (how are we perceived vs competitors?)
• Notable relationship moments (exec connections, partnership conversations, happy hours)
• Notable expansion, renewal, new-logo signals

Please also make sure Popl is updated with your notes by end of day so we can finalise reporting. Exact numbers help — counts, names of accounts, not "lots" or "several". We measure events by total impact, not just pipeline, so your insights help us decide whether to attend again!
```
