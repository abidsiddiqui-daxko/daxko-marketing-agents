## 11. WEBINAR LOGISTICS

A webinar has no booth, travel or shipping: skip those checklist items (mark them "n/a — webinar") and the Tier rule unless the person gives a budget or headcount. Registration page, invites, reminders, no-show and attendee follow-up emails and on-demand promotion are **copy → `daxko-event-promotion`**; this skill plans the logistics around them.

## 12. POST-EVENT RECAP + EVENT ROI SCORE

Read `references/event-roi-and-recap.md` in full first. Its rules apply word for word: **"We measure events by total brand and relationship value — not just pipeline."** · 100% of events get a report, published within 3–4 business days · the Salesforce campaign, members and opportunity ties are updated **before** the report is final · **numbers must be exact** — never "many", "several", "a couple"; if the notes give no specific number, quote or stat, leave it out · tables, checklists and scoring models; executive-ready, no inspirational tone.

**Evidence sources** (ask for what is missing, ≤3 per message): Popl export or counts (scans, MQL vs Visited Booth) · Salesforce campaign (leads, meetings, opportunities influenced, ARR influenced — **Salesforce is the source of truth for Demand Creation**) · team recap notes (week-of message 3) · social / content numbers · customer and partner meetings · cost (for cost per meeting / per opportunity).

**Recap — in this order:**
1. **Quantifiable ROI** (Demand Creation) — exact numbers as pasted; Salesforce incomplete → `Pending`
2. **Engagement ROI** (Relationship Equity) — customers met, testimonials recorded (approval → Agent 67 — Customer Proof & Advocacy), partner meetings
3. **Brand ROI** (Brand Impact) — reach, engagement, press, sentiment — as pasted
4. **Content value** (Content & Amplification) — assets created, engagement, traffic
5. **Long-term advocacy impact** (Community & Advocacy) — referrals, customer mentions, sign-ups
6. **Strategic score** — the table below
7. **Recommended portfolio action** — only when all five are scored

- Every score is labelled **"proposed — your judgement; Daxko's 1–5 anchor rubric is not on file"**.
- **Tier and action** only with a full total, from the file's table: 22–25 Portfolio anchor → **EXPAND** · 18–21 Strategic event → **KEEP** · 14–17 Solid execution → **KEEP with focus areas** · 10–13 Marginal → **EVALUATE / FIX** · under 10 Low ROI → **DROP**. The final call is the event manager's.
- **Older scores:** a past /20 score is the older 4-dimension model — say so and never compare it with a /25 score as if they were the same, never convert it.
- **Executive deck:** the Event ROI Matrix slide sits right after the event budget slide — a 3×3 matrix: top row quantifiable ROI (Demand Creation), middle engagement ROI (Relationship Equity), bottom brand ROI (Brand Impact); Content & Amplification and Community & Advocacy wrap around it.
- **Quarterly portfolio review (by market),** when asked: per event — total ROI score · cost per opportunity · cost per meeting · new logo vs expansion impact · velocity influence · open pipeline health → Add / Keep / Drop, each figure only as pasted and each division shown. Never recommend more events without ROI justification.
