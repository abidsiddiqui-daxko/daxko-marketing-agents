# Event ROI and post-event recap — how Daxko measures an event

## Core principle

Every event is measured across three macro axes: **Brand → Relationship → Revenue**. These expand into **five dimensions**, each scored 1–5.

> **"We measure events by total brand and relationship value — not just pipeline."**

Events drive five layers of ROI: **Awareness → Reputation → Relationships → Demand → Advocacy**.

## The five dimensions

| # | Dimension | Metric type | Example evidence | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Brand Impact** | Awareness & perception | Attendees reached · social reach and engagement lift · press mentions · influencer shares · branded content produced · sentiment improvement after the event | Social monitoring tools · content analytics · post-event brand surveys · earned media tracking |
| 2 | **Relationship Equity** | Customer & partner engagement | Existing customers met · testimonials recorded · partner meetings or collaborations started · customer stories captured · NPS change | CRM notes · event logs · customer marketing data · partner follow-up reports |
| 3 | **Demand Creation** | Pipeline influence | Leads captured · appointments booked · opportunities influenced · ARR influenced · CAC efficiency by event tier · attribution share in multi-touch journeys | Salesforce campaigns · opportunity attribution reporting · UTM tracking · post-event conversion rates |
| 4 | **Content & Amplification** | Owned media ROI | Video assets created · engagement from event content · website traffic from event content · evergreen content (blogs, reels, clips) | CMS analytics · YouTube data · campaign reporting · organic growth |
| 5 | **Community & Advocacy** | Long-term equity | Referrals from attendees · social mentions from customers · community sign-ups · expansion into new sub-verticals | Referral tracking · social listening · partner and influencer dashboards |

> **Salesforce is the source of truth for Demand Creation.** When Salesforce data is incomplete, mark it `Pending` — never estimate.

## Scoring

Each event gets a 1–5 score per dimension, added up to a total **out of 25**.

| Total | Tier of strategic value | Default recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| 22–25 | Portfolio anchor — non-replaceable | **EXPAND** |
| 18–21 | Strategic event — high value | **KEEP** |
| 14–17 | Solid execution — selective continuation | **KEEP with focus areas** |
| 10–13 | Marginal — needs a structural fix or repositioning | **EVALUATE / FIX** |
| under 10 | Low ROI — consider dropping | **DROP** |

**Not on file:** the "1–5 anchor rubric" (what earns a 1, 3 or 5 in each dimension). The source says it lives in "Project Instructions", which are not in Agent HQ. Until it is supplied, a score must show the evidence it rests on and be labelled as the person's or the skill's judgement.

**Historical note:** older scores used a 4-dimension / 20-point model (for example CrossFit Games 19/20, MAIACON 15/20, Two Brain Summit 15/20, GSDCon 9/20 — illustrative). The current standard is 5 dimensions / 25 points. Never compare a /20 score with a /25 score without saying so.

## How the recap is laid out (presentation standard)

Every post-event report follows this order:

1. Quantifiable ROI (hard metrics → Demand Creation)
2. Engagement ROI (Relationship Equity)
3. Brand ROI (awareness and perception)
4. Content value
5. Long-term advocacy impact
6. Strategic score (each dimension and the total out of 25)
7. Recommended portfolio action (Add / Keep / Drop, or Expand / Keep / Fix / Drop from the scoring table)

In an executive deck, the Event ROI Matrix slide sits **right after the event budget slide**. As a slide it is a 3×3 matrix: top row = quantifiable ROI (Demand Creation), middle = engagement ROI (Relationship Equity), bottom = brand ROI (Brand Impact); Content & Amplification and Community & Advocacy wrap around it.

## Reporting rules (from the recap instructions)

- **100% of events** get a post-event report.
- Reports are published **within 3–4 business days** of the event.
- Salesforce campaigns, members and opportunity ties are updated **before** the report is final.
- If someone describes an event with no report, prompt them to complete the ROI Matrix report.
- **Numbers must be exact.** Never write vague amounts ("many", "several", "a couple"). If the team notes give no specific number, quote or stat, leave it out — do not add vague detail.
- No overly inspirational tone. Tables, checklists and scoring models; executive-ready.

## Quarterly portfolio review (by market)

For each event: total ROI score · cost per opportunity · cost per meeting · new logo vs expansion impact · velocity influence · open pipeline health → **Add / Keep / Drop** recommendation. Never recommend more events without ROI justification.
