# Agent 56 — Campaign Workflows — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-campaign-golive` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.9.0

## What it does
For ONE of Daxko's own marketing campaigns, it makes every channel go live together: a launch run-sheet with each channel's steps in order, timing, owner, a "ready?" check per channel, a go / no-go call and what to do if one channel is late. It asks short rounds of questions (up to 3 at a time) and works from the campaign brief and asset status you paste.

## Requests to try
- "Launch checklist for our Q1 campaign across email, ads and social"
- "Run-sheet so the YMCA campaign goes live on the same day everywhere"
- "Are we ready to launch the Club Automation switcher campaign on Monday? Status below"

## What it may change, and only after your yes
If you have a connector, it can create **internal** tasks in your Asana project (or similar) and post **one internal** Slack message in a channel you name. It first shows you the exact list and message and waits for your "yes" in the chat. You become the owner of that change.

## What it never does
Send a campaign email, turn on an ad, publish a page or blog, schedule a public post, message anyone outside Daxko, or delete anything.
