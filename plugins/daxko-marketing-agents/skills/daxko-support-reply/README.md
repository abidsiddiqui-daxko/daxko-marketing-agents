# Agent 45 — CX Email & Response — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-support-reply` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.9.0

## What it does
Drafts one reply to one customer support email or case you paste — a billing question, a how-to, a bug, an upset customer — for you to check and send. It can also write one reusable response template, or fix a reply you already drafted.

## How it works
Daxko's files hold no support policies, so it first asks you (at most 3 short questions) for what is missing: the customer's message, the policy or help article that applies, and what you are allowed to offer. If the case history is in a connector you have, it asks permission before reading it — read-only, for that one case. Anything you paste is used for that chat only.

## Requests to try
- "Draft a reply to this upset YMCA director about a double charge" (paste the email)
- "Help me answer this Zen Planner ticket" (paste it)
- "Response template for login questions"
- "Fix my reply to this customer" (paste your draft)

## What you get
The reply under **DRAFT REPLY — needs the support agent to check it before it is sent**, a CHECK BEFORE SENDING list, internal notes, what it declined and who owns it, NOT ON FILE, the Key Result, one next step and sources with dates.

## Good to know
- It never promises a refund, credit, discount, price, fix date or response time unless you paste the approved policy or your manager's approval.
- It never confirms or dates roadmap features (AI Agents, Smart Sending Engine…).
- It leaves out names, emails, phones, card numbers, passwords and health details.
- It sends, closes and updates nothing — you send it.
