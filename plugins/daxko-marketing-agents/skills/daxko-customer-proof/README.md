# Agent 67 — Customer Proof & Advocacy

**Tell it which customer is happy and why, and it tells you what to ask them for — a review, a testimonial quote, a case study or a reference call — then drafts the ask for you to send.** It also tells you what customer proof Daxko is already allowed to use for each market.

**Business owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Skill name:** `daxko-customer-proof` · **Batch 2** · first published v1.7.23

## Requests to try
> Little Rock Athletic Club just renewed and their GM loves Engage Pro — can we get a case study out of them?

> Draft a testimonial request for a YMCA that just finished go-live.

> What approved customer proof can I use for martial arts studios?

> Who should I ask for a reference call for a JCC deal against Amilia?

## What you get back
1. **The ask** that fits, why, whether to ask **now or wait**, and whom to ask (by role)
2. **A draft message** with `[First name]` placeholders — you review and send it
3. **An interview guide** for case studies, crediting the customer's team
4. **An approval checklist** — what you can use today (exactly as written) and what needs the customer's written approval
5. **Proof on file** for the market, with each item's approval status
6. What isn't on file, the Key Result, one next step (usually a brand check with Agent 9 — Brand Guardian) and the files it read

## What it will NOT do
| Ask | Who owns it |
|---|---|
| Write the finished case study, story or testimonial page | `daxko-nonprofit-copywriter` · `club-automation-copywriter` · `zen-planner-copywriter` |
| A referral, refer-a-friend or ambassador program | `daxko-referral-program` |
| An email-only sequence | Agent 15 — Email & Nurture (`daxko-email-nurture`) |
| A multi-touch sequence with calls or LinkedIn | `gong-sequence-builder` |
| Research or churn interviews | `daxko-customer-research` |
| Competitor questions | Agent 3 — Market Intelligence & Competitive |
| Send anything, or log it in a CRM | You — it only drafts |

## Good to know
- It **never invents a quote or a number**, and turns down requests to "add a punchy quote".
- It says **wait** if the customer has an open ticket, billing dispute, migration or complaint.
- It never suggests a gift or incentive for a public review.
- Who signs off on Daxko's side isn't on file — it says "confirm who signs off".
- YMCA and Boys & Girls Club case studies are not on file yet; it says so rather than borrowing another segment's customer.
