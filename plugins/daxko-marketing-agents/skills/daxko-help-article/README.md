# Agent 46 — Knowledge Base & Article — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-help-article` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.9.0

## What it does
Writes one help-center article, FAQ or how-to for Daxko customers or their staff, from the product steps, release note or support answer you paste. It can also update an old article you paste and list every change.

## How it works
Daxko's files hold no product steps, so it first asks you (at most 3 short questions) for the steps or note, the product, version and reader, and where it will be posted. If the current article lives in a connector you have, it asks permission before reading it — read-only, for that one article. It never invents a menu, button, setting, limit or fix.

## Requests to try
- "Help article on setting up family billing in Daxko Operations from these steps" (paste them)
- "Turn this ticket answer into a knowledge base article" (paste it)
- "FAQ for Zen Planner belt promotions" (paste the answers)
- "Update this article now that this release note is out" (paste both)

## What you get
The article under **DRAFT ARTICLE — needs a product or support expert to check every step before it is published**, a GAPS TO FILL list, internal notes, what it declined and who owns it, NOT ON FILE, the Key Result, one next step and sources with dates.

## Good to know
- Steps, screen and button names are kept exactly as you pasted them.
- Roadmap features (AI Agents, Smart Sending Engine…) and beta items are never written as available.
- No prices or plan claims unless you paste them.
- It publishes nothing — who publishes is not on file, so confirm it.
