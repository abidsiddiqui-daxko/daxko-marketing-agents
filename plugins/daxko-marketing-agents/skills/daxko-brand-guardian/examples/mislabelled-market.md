# Example — the label is wrong, the copy is fine

This is the example that stops the most damaging kind of mistake this reviewer can make: **rejecting
good work for the wrong reason, with a correct citation next to every failure.**

A submitter picks the wrong market far more often than they write the wrong copy. Read the evidence,
not the dropdown.

---

## The submitted draft

> **Market:** club — commercial gyms · **Type:** copy
>
> Your Y's mission deserves better. For 150 years your branches have served families across the
> community, and Daxko Operations helps your team honour that mission every day. Board-ready reporting
> means your directors see community outcomes, not spreadsheets. Together, we strengthen the
> communities you serve.
>
> Talk to our team about your gym.

---

## The review

**Reviewing:** copy · **Market:** **nonprofit — inferred from the content, not from the label.**
The submitter labelled this `club — commercial gyms`, but the copy addresses a YMCA's mission, its
branches, its board and the community it serves. Every one of those is nonprofit-native. I reviewed
against nonprofit. If the club label is genuinely correct, see the note at the end — the verdict
changes completely and the piece needs rewriting rather than editing.

## Verdict: REJECTED

*(One dimension `fail`s, so the verdict is REJECTED — one failure is enough, however good the rest is.
The two violations are quick edits, but "quick to fix" never softens a `fail` into a `warning`. Both
are absolute nonprofit bans.)*

## Scorecard

| Dimension | Result | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Voice and tone | pass | Mission-first, board-trust framing, community outcomes — correct nonprofit register |
| Terminology and naming | fail | "Together, we strengthen…" is a banned "[x], together" construction; "gym" is banned in nonprofit content |
| Visual identity | pass | not assessable — no visual elements |
| Audience fit | pass | Speaks to a YMCA executive and board in their own language — mission, branches, directors |
| Claims and proof | pass | No Daxko performance claim is made, so none requires sourcing |
| Call-to-action clarity | pass | One CTA, "Talk to our team" is an approved replacement, no urgency |

## Violations

**1. Banned "together" construction**
- **Quoted:** "Together, we strengthen the communities you serve."
- **Rule:** "Never use in NP content: … '[x], together' (any 'together' construction)" — `references/brand-guidelines.md`; repeated in `references/banned-words.md` and as Operational Guideline 3 in `references/nonprofit-playbook.md`.
- **Why it matters:** It is an absolute nonprofit ban, and it is the closing line — the most-read sentence after the opener.

**2. "Gym" in nonprofit content**
- **Quoted:** "Talk to our team about your gym."
- **Rule:** "gym" is banned in NP content — `references/brand-guidelines.md`. Replacements: facility, branch, center, location — `references/banned-words.md`.
- **Why it matters:** A YMCA does not have a gym; it has branches and facilities. The word signals the writer does not know the sector.

## Suggested edits

| Line | Currently | Change to |
|---|---|---|
| Close | "Together, we strengthen the communities you serve." | "Your branches serve stronger communities, and your board can see it." |
| CTA | "Talk to our team about your gym." | "Talk to our team about your branches." |

## Corrected rewrite

> Your Y's mission deserves better. For 150 years your branches have served families across the
> community, and Daxko Operations helps your team honour that mission every day. Board-ready reporting
> means your directors see community outcomes, not spreadsheets.
>
> Talk to our team about your branches.

## What this example is demonstrating

| | |
|---|---|
| **Evidence beats the label** | The content decides the market. The label is a hint that can be wrong |
| **The mismatch is stated in one line at the top** | The submitter learns their label is the problem, not their writing |
| **Both readings are given** | The "if the club label is correct" table lets a human settle it in one reply, instead of a second round of review |
| **`150 years` is NOT flagged** | It is the customer's own history, not a Daxko performance claim. Only Daxko's figures need a bundled source — `references/brand-guidelines.md` covers claims about Daxko, not facts about the customer |
| **Mission language is NOT flagged** | In nonprofit it is *required*. The same words would be a `fail` in club. This is why getting the market right comes before everything else |
| **Two real violations, not four false ones** | The pre-fix version of this skill rejected this draft on four dimensions for using mission language, with a correct file citation on each. Every citation made the wrong answer look unarguable |

**The failure mode this exists to prevent:** a colleague reads four cited failures, believes them, and
strips the mission out of a YMCA email — producing copy that breaks the *actual* nonprofit rules the
reviewer is supposed to protect.
