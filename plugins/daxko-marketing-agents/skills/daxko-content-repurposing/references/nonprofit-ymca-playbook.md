## YMCA-Specific ICP Notes

### Buying Committee
- **Structure:** Multi-layered, consensus-driven
- **Sponsor:** CEO must sponsor the initiative for it to move forward
- **Approval:** Board approval required for major technology investments
- **Influencers:** Branch directors influence but don't decide; IT/technology directors evaluate but don't fund
- **Peer influence:** YMCA of the USA recommendations carry significant weight; regional network referrals accelerate deals

### Y-USA Strategic Alliance
- Daxko maintains a strategic alliance partnership with YMCA of the USA
- This is a competitive moat — leverage Y-USA endorsement in all YMCA campaigns
- Reference Y-USA alignment in executive-level conversations

### Budget Timing
- Fiscal year varies by association: calendar year OR July-June
- Most budget decisions made in Q4 for calendar-year orgs, Q2 for July-June orgs
- Always confirm fiscal year before campaign timing

### Procurement
- RFP process required above certain thresholds (varies by org)
- Larger YMCAs may involve formal evaluation committees with scoring rubrics
- Multi-year contracts are common; switching costs are high

### Enrichment Signals (for ABM targeting)
- New CEO / COO / CIO appointment
- Facility expansions or capital campaigns
- Digital transformation mentioned in strategic plans or board minutes
- Active RFPs or vendor evaluation announcements
- Job postings for technology roles

## Messaging Do's and Don'ts (YMCA-Specific)
- **Do:** Reference community results, member outcomes, member experience, mission alignment. ⚠️ **Do NOT write the word "impact" — it is banned for Nonprofit in `banned-words.md`, which supplies these replacements.** *(corrected 2026-09-22 — found by Agent 13 — Content Production's test suite)*
- **Do:** Use "association" language (YMCAs call their network "associations")
- **Do:** Mention Y-USA strategic alliance where appropriate
- **Do:** Frame operational efficiency as enabling more mission delivery
- **Don't:** Lead with cost savings alone — lead with mission enablement
- **Don't:** Compare to commercial gym software — YMCAs are nonprofits first
- **Don't:** Assume all YMCAs have the same fiscal year or budget process

## Competitive Positioning (YMCA Context)

### vs. TractionRec
- **Their pitch:** Salesforce-native, enterprise CRM integration
- **Their weakness:** Salesforce governance burden, steeper learning curve, expanding TCO over time, requires dedicated Salesforce admin
- **Our counter:** "Who owns Salesforce admin, testing, and ongoing tuning?" — Position Daxko as purpose-built operations vs. CRM-configured governance burden
- **Win with:** Vendor-managed simplicity, Y-USA strategic alliance, purpose-built for nonprofit wellness (not configured from a general-purpose CRM)

### vs. ACTIVENet
- **Their pitch:** Parks and rec heritage, government/municipal integrations
- **Their weakness:** Complex pricing, hidden fees, fragmented data, not purpose-built for nonprofits
- **Our counter:** Win on transparency, integrated data, and purpose-built nonprofit operations
- **Win with:** Transparent pricing, unified platform, nonprofit-specific workflows

### vs. Amilia
- **Their pitch:** Modern registration platform, user-friendly interface
- **Their weakness:** Registration-only — no real-time data, no built-in fundraising, no risk management
- **Our counter:** Lead with real-time data, built-in fundraising integration, and risk management — these are critical gaps for YMCAs
- **Win with:** Full-platform depth vs. point-solution limitations

### Common Switching Triggers
- Legacy system end-of-life or vendor acquisition
- New executive leadership seeking modernization
- Failed implementation with a competitor
- Y-USA recommendation or peer referral
- Need for integrated fundraising + membership data
