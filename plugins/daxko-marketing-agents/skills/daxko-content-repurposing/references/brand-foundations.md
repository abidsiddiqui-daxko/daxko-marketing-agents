### Typography

| Use | Typeface |
|-----|----------|
| Primary brand typeface | Barlow |
| Fallback (when Barlow unavailable) | Arial |

### Logo Usage Rules

**Color logo permitted backgrounds:**
- White
- Black
- Lightest tints (050, 100) and darkest shades (950, 900) of Flex Gray only

**Brand color backgrounds:**
- Always use the fully white logo on any brand color background

**Logo don'ts:**
- No low contrast
- No unapproved colors
- No rearranged or resized elements
- No shadows or effects
- No color logo on a background that already contains a logo color

### Solution Naming Rules
- Use the full branded name on first headline and first body copy usage — e.g. "Daxko Payments" not "Payments"
- Use the full product name "Club Automation" (not "CA") in first headline and first body copy instance
- Solution logo for products requiring login; Daxko logo + written names when referencing multiple solutions
- For co-branded or partner contexts (e.g. SugarWOD integrations), the Daxko logo always takes precedence. No co-branding without design approval.

### Casing Standards

| Use | Casing |
|-----|--------|
| Headings, subheadings, body copy, UI labels | Sentence case |
| CTAs, product names, approved design exceptions | Title case |

## 4. Approved Boilerplate (Use Verbatim)

These are pre-approved copy assets. Do NOT paraphrase. Use exactly as written.

### Company Boilerplate

> Daxko powers the world's leading fitness and wellness organizations, providing innovative technology that maximizes productivity, increases engagement, and drives sustainable growth. With a comprehensive suite of software solutions, including operations automation, payment processing, and member engagement tools, Daxko helps businesses scale with confidence. Backed by deep industry expertise and a commitment to long-term success, Daxko partners with fitness clubs, boutique studios, YMCAs, and wellness centers to create thriving communities where individuals can reach their full potential.

### LinkedIn Bio

> At Daxko, we power the world's leading fitness and wellness businesses—helping them maximize productivity, increase engagement, and drive sustainable growth. From seamless operations to data-driven insights, our technology empowers organizations to create thriving communities where people reach their full potential.

### Twitter / X Bio

> We power the world's leading fitness & wellness brands. Maximize productivity, engage members, and scale with confidence.

> **Note on banned words in boilerplate:** The approved boilerplate above contains "innovative", "seamless", and "empowers" — words that appear in `06-banned-words.md` as banned for general use. The boilerplate is an explicit exception: it is the official approved version and must not be edited even to remove banned words. If the boilerplate itself needs updating, route to Anna Klement (anna.klement@daxko.com). For all NON-boilerplate content, the banned-words list applies.
