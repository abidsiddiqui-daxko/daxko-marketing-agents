# Agent 71 — Budget Reallocation — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-budget-reallocation` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.7.37

## What it does
Recommends how to move a Digital Services client's ad budget between campaigns or channels (Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn). You paste spend and results for one period — or, if you say yes, it reads them from a connector you already have. It checks the numbers add up, works out what each lead or join costs, flags thin data, and gives a move-by-move table, what to watch, and a short summary for the client.

## Requests to try
- "Should we shift budget from Meta to Google for this YMCA client?" (paste the numbers)
- "Which of this client's campaigns should get more spend?"
- "Rebalance this club client's monthly ad budget"

## Good to know
- It recommends only. It never moves money, changes bids or budgets, or pauses ads — a person does that after the client agrees.
- No benchmarks or "industry averages" unless you paste them. Few results = low confidence, said plainly.
- It never promises the client Daxko's own Digital Marketing Services results.
- Lead lists with names or emails: it works from counts only.
