## Primary Competitors in Club Market

### ABC Fitness
- **Position:** Full-service payment facilitator (payfac) for large franchises and multi-location operators
- **Strengths:**
  - Next-day ACH funding
  - Strong franchise management capabilities
  - Established brand recognition in the club space
  - Deep franchise billing infrastructure
- **Weaknesses:**
  - 7-10 day delay for billing changes to take effect
  - Slow customer support response times
  - No dynamic or real-time reporting
  - Expensive API access — limits integrations
  - Mobile app sync issues across locations
- **Win themes vs. ABC:**
  - Self-service flexibility — make changes instantly, not in 7-10 days
  - Speed of change — real-time billing adjustments vs. week-plus delays
  - Real-time reporting — see revenue and member data as it happens
  - Open API — integrate without punitive costs
  - Unified mobile experience — consistent across all locations
- **Win patterns (CL-07 Q6):** When clubs want control and flexibility in how they run their business. Operators frustrated with delayed changes, limited financial visibility, or fragmented billing processes consistently choose Club Automation for real-time control, unified billing, and stronger day-to-day operations.
- **Loss patterns (CL-07 Q7):** When buyers prioritize simplicity or are already standardized on ABC's payfac model. Typically large franchise environments or lower-complexity clubs where hands-off billing and brand familiarity outweigh the need for flexibility and operational control.
- **Trap question:** *"How much control do you actually have today over your billing, refunds, and when your funds hit your account?"*

### Mindbody (Club)
- **Position:** ~60K businesses globally, primarily studio and spa-first platform expanding into clubs
- **Strengths:**
  - Strong consumer brand and marketplace
  - ClassPass integration and partnership
  - AI features in premium pricing tiers
  - Large installed base creates switching inertia
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Lacks club-specific depth — built for studios, not full-service clubs
  - Manual workarounds required for family memberships
  - Add-on pricing inflates total cost significantly
  - 20% marketplace fee per new client acquired through the marketplace
  - No native access control hardware integration
- **Win themes vs. Mindbody:**
  - Purpose-built for clubs — not a studio platform with club workarounds
  - Transparent pricing — no surprise add-on costs
  - Native access control — integrated hardware, not third-party bolt-ons
  - Complex membership support — family memberships, corporate accounts, tiered pricing out of the box
- **Win patterns (CL-07 Q21):** When clubs outgrow basic class scheduling and need to manage real operations. Multi-amenity facilities, family memberships, and multi-location reporting all expose Mindbody's limitations and highlight the depth of Club Automation.
- **Loss patterns (CL-07 Q22):** When the buyer is a boutique or studio-focused business prioritizing simplicity, price, or marketplace exposure. In these cases, operational depth matters less than quick setup and consumer-facing tools.
- **Trap question:** *"How are you managing family memberships or non-class offerings like open gym, facility access, or aquatics?"*

### Zenoti
- **Position:** Enterprise spa and salon platform ($1B valuation), actively entering the fitness market
- **Strengths:**
  - Modern, polished UI
  - AI assistant "Zeenie" for automated tasks
  - Strong marketing automation tools
  - Well-funded (VC-backed) with aggressive growth strategy
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Spa-first architecture — no native facility scheduling, no batch EFT processing, no family membership grouping
  - Steep learning curve for non-spa use cases
  - Opaque pricing model — difficult to get clear total cost
  - Known UI glitches and performance issues reported by users
  - Fitness-specific workflows require extensive customization
- **Win themes vs. Zenoti:**
  - Purpose-built for clubs — not a spa platform being retrofitted for fitness
  - Configurable out of the box — no expensive customization required
  - Fitness-specialist onboarding — implementation team that understands club operations
  - Transparent pricing — know your total cost upfront
- **Win patterns (CL-07 Q38):** When clubs need a system built specifically for their operations. Multi-purpose facilities, complex memberships, and teams that value usability and support consistently choose Club Automation over a more generic, spa-first platform.
- **Loss patterns (CL-07 Q39):** When buyers are drawn to modern UI or AI features early in the process, or when spa-style operations are a core part of the business. In these cases, perceived innovation can outweigh operational fit.
- **Trap question:** *"How are you handling facility scheduling, open gym time, or court reservations without workarounds?"*

### Jonas Fitness
- **Position:** Legacy platform serving YMCAs, wellness centers, and traditional fitness facilities
- **Strengths:**
  - Long industry tenure and established relationships
  - Outsourced billing and collections service
  - Robust check-in and access control systems
  - Childcare management capabilities
  - Welld Health integration for medical fitness
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Frequent downtime and system crashes
  - Complex, unintuitive workflows that require extensive training
  - Manual billing processes — limited automation
  - No native mobile app for members
  - Difficult and inflexible reporting
  - Long customer support hold times
- **Win themes vs. Jonas:**
  - Cloud platform with 99.9998% uptime vs. frequent crashes
  - Automated billing that eliminates manual work
  - Native mobile app for members and staff
  - Real-time, flexible reporting
  - Responsive customer support
- **CA honest limitations vs. Jonas:**
  - CA is newer in the medical fitness space (Jonas has Welld Health)
  - CA does not offer an outsourced billing/collections service
  - CA has stricter childcare management rules
- **Win patterns (CL-07 Q54):** When clubs want a modern, flexible system that reduces manual work. Operators moving off Jonas are typically frustrated with legacy workflows, limited usability, and the need for workarounds — choosing Club Automation for a more intuitive, configurable platform built for how clubs operate today.
- **Loss patterns (CL-07 Q55):** When clubs are deeply entrenched in Jonas and hesitant to switch from a long-standing system. Most common in organizations that prioritize familiarity and stability over modernization, or where switching costs feel too high.
- **Trap question:** *"How much manual work or workarounds does your team still rely on day-to-day just to keep operations running?"*

## Competitive Trends

### 2026 Competitive Shifts (CL-07 Q57, Apr 2026)
Most competitors are investing heavily in AI, automation, and surface-level innovation to expand their appeal. ABC Fitness and Zenoti are pushing AI-driven features, but these are often layered onto systems that weren't built for complex club operations — creating gaps between what looks advanced and what actually works day-to-day.

### Emerging Market Direction (CL-07 Q58, Apr 2026)
The market is shifting toward connected, intelligent platforms that support the full member journey. While AI is becoming more prominent, clubs are prioritizing solutions that combine automation with operational depth — ensuring new capabilities actually improve billing, retention, and staff efficiency, not just add complexity.

### Trend Summary
1. **AI and automation arms race** — Competitors adding surface-level AI features (Zenoti's Zeenie, Mindbody premium AI). Mostly marketing sizzle, not deep operational intelligence. CA's automation is purpose-built for club operations
2. **ABC frustration creating opening** — Growing dissatisfaction with ABC's 7-10 day change delays and support responsiveness. Active switching market for clubs that want more control
3. **Legacy platform modernization** — Jonas and similar legacy platforms showing age. Frequent downtime, manual workflows, and poor mobile experience creating modernization demand. Strong replacement cycle opportunity

## Competitive Opportunities — Where CA Wins Now

These are the three highest-confidence opening narratives as of April 2026 (from Kim's SME input, CL-07):

**ABC Fitness frustration:** Clubs using ABC are increasingly frustrated by lack of financial control, fragmented billing, and slow support. Win on control, transparency, and day-to-day usability. Lead with: "How much control do you actually have over your billing, refunds, and when your funds hit your account?"

**Mindbody and Zenoti growth ceiling:** Both platforms invest heavily in AI and marketplaces, but are not built for complex club operations. As clubs grow beyond simple scheduling, they hit walls with memberships, reporting, and facility management. **Jonas modernization wave:** Operators on Jonas are ready to move. Frustration with legacy workflows, limited usability, and manual workarounds is the primary trigger. Focus on operational depth, financial control, and a unified platform that works in real time.

## 2026 Updates

### Mindbody — ClassPass Acquisition Note
Mindbody acquired ClassPass — gyms reliant on ClassPass traffic for new member discovery may feel locked into the Mindbody ecosystem. ZP/CA ClassPass integration still exists independently, but this is a perception battle worth addressing in competitive conversations.

**Updated Mindbody win angle:** Beyond the 20% marketplace fee, some gyms now face a strategic dependency on a competitor's platform for acquisition traffic. Position CA as full ownership of the member acquisition stack — no third-party dependency.

### Zenoti — Updated Win/Lose Conditions (from CL-08)
**Win when (additional):** Prospect needs multi-purpose facilities management, complex family memberships, or court/swim lane scheduling — Zenoti's spa-first architecture cannot handle these natively without expensive customization.

**Lose when (additional):** Buyer already drawn to Zenoti's modern UI or AI assistant "Zeenie" early in the process, or when spa/wellness components are a significant part of the business.

**Updated trap question:** *"How do you currently manage family memberships or facility reservations like courts and swim lanes?"*

### Jonas — Strengths to Be Aware Of (from CL-08)
Previously understated Jonas strengths that come up in deals:
- **Check-in richness:** Jonas check-in screens display member photo, notes, and full account info on one screen — richer at-a-glance view than CA currently offers
- **Childcare detail:** Time-in-studio tracking + parent phone numbers visible at check-in · Flexible same-day childcare bookings (1-hr and 2-hr blocks)
- **Welld Health integration:** Physician referrals and insurance reimbursement programs — significant for medical fitness and hospital-affiliated clubs

**CA honest limitations vs. Jonas (updated):**
- CA is newer in medical fitness — Jonas has Welld Health depth
- CA does not offer outsourced billing/collections service (Jonas Member Billing Services with call centre)
- CA has stricter childcare booking rules vs. Jonas flexibility
- Jonas check-in screens show richer at-a-glance member information

> Never hide these limitations — address proactively and pivot to CA strengths (uptime, mobile, modern UX, automated billing).
