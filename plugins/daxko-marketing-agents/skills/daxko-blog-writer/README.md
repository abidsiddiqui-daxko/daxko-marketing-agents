# Agent 12 — Blog & Article — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-blog-writer` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.7.35

## What it does
Writes one search-optimized blog post or article for one market and one segment, from Daxko's files and Daxko's own blog guidelines (the SEO Content Guidelines and the Nonprofit, Club Automation and Zen Planner Blog Optimization Frameworks). You get title options, a meta description, the post with headings and FAQs, one demo call to action and link placeholders. It can also outline a post first, turn one pasted transcript into one post, or refresh an old post you paste and list every fix.

## Requests to try
- "Write a blog post for mid-size YMCAs about keeping members after January"
- "SEO blog for martial arts school owners on no-shows — our keyword is 'martial arts attendance' at 320 searches a month"
- "Refresh this old JCC blog post so it's accurate" (paste it)
- "Outline a blog post for club operators about failed payments first"

## What you get
A metadata block, the post under **DRAFT COPY** (needing Agent 9 — Brand Guardian before it goes out) kept apart from internal notes, SEO notes, proof gaps, a NOT ON FILE list and sources with dates.

## Good to know
- It uses keyword and traffic numbers only when you paste them (the only volumes on file are the Club Automation keyword table). It never claims "#1" or a ranking.
- It never invents customers, quotes, statistics, prices or trials, and never offers roadmap AI (AI Agents, Smart Sending Engine) as available, not even as "coming".
- Daxko's core brand, product and banned-words files win over the blog guideline files when they disagree.
- It writes and publishes nothing — no WordPress, no scheduling. Byline and sign-off are not on file: confirm them.
