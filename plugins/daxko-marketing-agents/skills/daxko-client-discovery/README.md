# Agent 27 — Strategy & Discovery — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-client-discovery` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.10.1

## What it does
Helps Daxko's Digital Services team understand one client — a YMCA, JCC, Boys & Girls Clubs, club or studio whose website or marketing Daxko builds or runs. It writes the discovery-session questions, compares the client's site with its own local competitors from pages you paste, or turns your discovery notes into a short, prioritised digital strategy. It never searches the web and never invents the client's facts, market numbers or anything about a competitor.

## Requests to try
- "Discovery questions for a new BGC website client"
- "Compare our client's site with these two local clubs" (paste the three pages)
- "Digital strategy recommendations from these discovery notes" (paste the notes)

## What you get
If something is missing, up to 3 short questions first — nothing else. Then the guide, comparison table or strategy under **DRAFT — needs the project lead before it goes out**, kept apart from internal notes, plus what it declined and who owns it, a NOT ON FILE list, the nearest Key Result and sources with dates.

## Good to know
- Daxko's files hold nothing about any client. Everything about the client and its competitors comes from what you paste, marked "as pasted", for that chat only.
- It can read your discovery notes through a connector you already have (Google Drive, Gong, Airtable), but only after asking you, read-only, for that one task.
- Recommendations are labelled for the project lead and client to decide, tied to a line you pasted, and never promise a result. Daxko's Digital Services proof is never presented as the client's.
- It compares on merits — no "scam" or attack lines — and uses no market statistic you did not paste with a source.
- It leaves out names, emails, phones, passwords and card details if you paste them. It sends, publishes and changes nothing. Who signs off is not on file: confirm it.
