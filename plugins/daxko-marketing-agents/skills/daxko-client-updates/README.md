# Agent 26 — Client Communication — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-client-updates` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.10.0

## What it does
Drafts one message from Daxko's Digital Services team to a website or marketing client about their project: a weekly or monthly status update, a meeting recap with decisions and action items, a reply to the client's question, or a request for content, access or approval. It works from the notes, call transcript or email thread you paste. If something is missing it first asks up to 3 short questions.

## Requests to try
- "Write this week's status update for our YMCA website client" (paste your notes)
- "Turn these kickoff notes into a recap email" (paste the notes)
- "The client asked why launch slipped — draft a reply" (paste the thread and the facts)
- "Ask the client for their photos and site logins"

## What you get
SCOPE, the message under **DRAFT — needs the project lead before it goes out** (subject, greeting, body, sign-off), internal notes kept apart (where each line came from, scope-creep and date risks), a NOT ON FILE list and sources with dates.

## Good to know
- Dates, results, prices and promises appear only as you pasted them. An unconfirmed date stays [needs: confirmed date].
- It never presents Daxko's own Digital Marketing Services results as the client's, and never blames the client or a teammate.
- It never asks a client to email a password: "use the secure way your project lead sets up".
- It sends nothing and posts nothing to Asana. It reads a connector only if you say yes, read-only. Sign-off is not on file: confirm it.
