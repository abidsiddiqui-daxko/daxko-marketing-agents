# Agent 51 — Roadmap & Release Briefing

**Paste a release note and it tells you what changed, who it matters to, and what you may and may NOT say about it yet. Ask "has X shipped?" and it tells you the delivery status Daxko's files hold.** Everything it writes is marked INTERNAL.

**Business owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Skill name:** `daxko-release-briefing` · **Batch 2** · first published v1.7.24

## Requests to try
> Here's the Club Automation 6/9/2026 release note — brief the sales team on what changed and what we can and can't say about it yet.

> What's the delivery status of AI Agents and Member Intelligence 360 — can I tell a YMCA prospect they're available?

> For the customer webinar, what can we say about the roadmap, and what has to stay internal?

> Zen Planner pushed an update this week — give customer success a what-changed summary and what we shouldn't promise.

## What you get back
1. **What changed**, item by item, each with its **delivery status** (in production, coming, or not confirmed)
2. **Which product and market** it belongs to — and a flag if the note has it wrong
3. **Who it matters to**, by role
4. **May say today / May NOT say yet** — side by side
5. Any place the note **contradicts Daxko's files**, shown side by side — never silently fixed
6. What isn't on file, the Key Result, one next step, and the files it read

## Good to know
- It works from **the note you paste** and Daxko's own files. It has no live roadmap tool.
- **AI Agents and Member Intelligence 360°:** the files said "Q3 2026", that quarter has closed, and delivery is **not confirmed**. It will never call them available.
- Smart Sending Engine, Dynamic Segmentation and Intelligent Scoring are "Early 2027, directional" — never available today.
- It never repeats personal contact details you paste.
- Who confirms delivery status is not on file — it says "confirm who signs off".
