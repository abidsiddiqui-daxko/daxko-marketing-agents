# Agent 50 — Product Insights & Feedback

**Paste customer feedback — NPS comments, survey answers, support tickets, review text, call notes — and it tells you what customers are really saying, how sure it is, and what Daxko should do about it.** It is the Daxko version of general customer research: every theme comes back tied to the right product, its delivery status and the Key Result it moves.

**Business owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Skill name:** `daxko-feedback-insights` · **Batch 2** · first published v1.7.25

## Requests to try
> What are customers telling us? [paste NPS comments]

> Make sense of these support tickets — what are clubs struggling with?

> Here are 20 survey answers from Zen Planner studios. Voice of customer, please.

> Top feature requests from this list — which ones do we already have?

## What you get back
1. **Ranked themes** with counts ("4 of 11") and a confidence label (High / Medium / Low)
2. For each theme: **bug, confusion, missing feature or praise**, the **Daxko product and market**, and whether it is **already delivered, on the roadmap or new**
3. Whether it matches a **known weakness** already in Daxko's files
4. **Who it matters to** (by role) and the **Key Result** it moves
5. A few short quotes, with names removed, marked "not an approved quote"
6. What the feedback can't tell you, one next step, and the files it read

## Good to know
- Please remove names and emails before pasting. If any slip through, it removes them and never repeats them. It won't take a full CRM or survey export.
- It never promises a roadmap feature. Requests for AI Agents or Smart Sending come back as internal notes only.
- It never turns a happy comment into a website quote — that needs the customer's written approval (Agent 67 — Customer Proof & Advocacy).
