# Agent 34 — SEO & AEO — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-site-seo` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.8.0

## What it does
Gives one website — a Digital Services client's by default, Daxko's own if you say so — an ordered list of fixes so it shows up better on Google (SEO) and in AI answers like ChatGPT (AEO). You paste Search Console or Google Analytics numbers for one period (or, if you say yes, it reads them from a connector you already have). It checks the numbers add up, works out click rates, and says what to fix first, why, how big a job, who does it and what to watch — plus a short summary for the client.

## Requests to try
- "SEO fixes for this YMCA client's site from this Search Console export" (paste the numbers)
- "Why did this club client's organic traffic drop?" (paste two periods)
- "Get this client's pages into AI answers" (paste the page text)

## Good to know
- It recommends only. It never edits the site, the CMS or Search Console — a person does that after the client agrees.
- It can't see the live site or what ChatGPT shows. Keyword and ranking numbers only if you paste them.
- It never promises a ranking, "#1" or an AI citation, and never promises Daxko's own marketing results to a client.
- Rules of thumb are labelled "general practice, not a Daxko rule".
