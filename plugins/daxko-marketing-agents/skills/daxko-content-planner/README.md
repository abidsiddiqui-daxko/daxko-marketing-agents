# Agent 10 — Content Strategy — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-content-planner` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.7.30

## What it does
Plans what to publish for one market or segment from Daxko's own files: a content plan or editorial calendar, a list of topic ideas, a gap check against content you paste, or a ranking of topics using numbers you paste. Every topic shows where it came from. It says "not on file" when the files are silent.

## Requests to try
- "Content plan for Q1 for Boys & Girls Clubs"
- "Blog topics for JCC finance directors"
- "What content are we missing for martial arts?" (paste your list of titles)
- "Rank these topics" (paste Search Console numbers)

## What you get
A plan with a source for every topic, proof gaps, timing placeholders (confirm the dates), the Key Result, a NOT ON FILE list and sources with dates.

## Good to know
- It never invents keywords, search volumes, rankings, event dates or customer stories. If you want a ranking, paste the numbers; it cites them back.
- It cannot see what is already published, so paste your titles for a gap check.
- It never offers roadmap AI (AI Agents, Member Intelligence 360°) as a topic that is available, not even as "coming".
- It writes no posts and schedules nothing. Who signs off a plan is not on file: confirm who signs off.
