## Functional Fitness Segment Overview
- **Sub-segments:** CrossFit boxes, F45 Studios, HIIT gyms, boot camp, strength & conditioning, personal trainers, fitness influencers / online coaches
- **Current Daxko penetration:** 5.5%
- **Penetration target (2026):** 7%
- **Sales cycle:** Days to weeks — owner decides, no committee
- **Decision maker:** Box/gym owner, often a former athlete or certified trainer (for studios) · Individual coach/creator (for trainers and influencers)
- **Primary product (Q3 2026+):** Exercise.com (with SugarWOD integration where applicable)
- **Transition platform (pre-Q3 2026):** Zen Planner + SugarWOD

## Functional Fitness ICP Notes
- **Owner profile:** Fitness enthusiast turned entrepreneur, often certified trainer or former competitor
- **Business maturity:** 1-2 locations, 100-300 members, $150K-$500K revenue
- **Key differentiator from MA:** More trend-driven, higher churn, more competitive market — new studios open constantly
- **Community focus:** Community is the #1 retention driver — programming matters less than belonging
- **Tech expectation:** Modern, mobile-first, integrates with workout tracking tools

## Proof Points

### CrossFit 1926 — Flex Fees Success Story
- **Result:** $10,690.08 in total revenue growth, of which **Flex Fees contributed $2,404.41 (22.5%)** *(corrected 2026-09-22 — found by Agent 13 — Content Production's test suite)*
- **Context:** Achieved DESPITE a 4% membership decline
- **How:** Flex Fees passes card and ACH transaction costs to the member at the point of payment, so the studio's margin stops absorbing the processing fee. **It does NOT recover failed payments** — that is Revenue Recovery Services / Zen Planner Revenue Recovery, a separate product *(corrected 2026-09-22 — found by Agent 13 — Content Production's test suite)*
- **Talk track:** "CrossFit 1926 grew revenue by $10,690 even while membership dropped 4%.
  How? Flex Fees caught the failed payments that were silently bleeding revenue every month.
  Most gym owners don't even know how much they're losing to payment failures."

### Why This Story Works
- Quantified result ($10,690 — real number, not a percentage)
- Counter-intuitive (revenue UP while members DOWN)
- Addresses a hidden pain point (payment failures most owners don't track)
- Applies to every functional fitness gym (they all have payment failures)

## Messaging Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Emphasize community-building tools and member engagement
- Speak to the competitive pressure from new studios opening nearby
- Lead with SugarWOD when talking to CrossFit/functional fitness — it's the hook
- Use the Campaign Marketplace as the growth differentiator
- Quantify results with real numbers (CrossFit 1926: $10,690)
- Address the "wearing every hat" reality — time savings matters most

### Don't
- Ignore franchise dynamics — always ask about franchisor requirements
- Position as "just billing software" — these owners need engagement + retention + growth tools
- Lead with features — lead with outcomes and time savings
- Assume CrossFit and F45 are the same — different cultures, different needs
- Promise things SugarWOD doesn't do — stay honest about capabilities

### Competitive Updates — 2026

#### PushPress — Elevated Threat
- **$20M Series B raised** — now marketing itself as AI-first in 2026
- **AI Assistant live in beta** — text-prompt-based member management; growing CrossFit community excitement
- **Updated win angle:** Campaign Marketplace is tangible (100+ campaigns, 2-click setup, proven 33% membership growth in 90 days) vs. PushPress AI which is conceptual and in beta
- **Updated lose condition:** Prospects already excited by PushPress AI-first vision are harder to convert
- **ZP/Exercise.com vulnerability:** ZP Capterra reviews (bugs, slowness, registration issues) are visible. G2 shows PushPress rated better on ongoing product support. Address proactively — "we heard you" messaging.
- **Updated trap questions:** "When a member asks what app to use to track workouts and PRs — what does PushPress give you to answer that?" / "Walk me through how you'd run a Facebook ad campaign from inside PushPress today."

#### Wodify — Onboarding Gap Closing
- **Improved onboarding (2026):** Wodify now offers 30-60-90 day check-ins — they are closing the gap on ZP's dedicated onboarding advantage
- **Still wins for ZP/Exercise.com when:** New client acquisition needed · Digital ads needed · Campaign Marketplace needed · Multi-vertical gym
- **Updated trap question:** "If you bring in 20 new Instagram leads this month — show me how Wodify converts them into paid members. What's automated, and what's manual?"

#### Mindbody — Active Switcher Pool
- **Removed all pricing from website in 2026** — "talk to sales" required; $139-599+/month opaque + 20% marketplace fee
- **Opportunity:** Large active switcher pool — gyms frustrated with pricing opacity
- **Win angle:** Exercise.com/ZP transparent pricing vs. Mindbody opacity + 20% marketplace fee
- **ClassPass note:** Mindbody acquired ClassPass — gyms reliant on ClassPass for discovery may feel locked in. ZP's ClassPass integration still exists independently.
- **Trap question:** "If you turned off the Mindbody consumer app tomorrow, how would you replace those leads — and what's your plan when they raise the fee?"

#### WellnessLiving — Add to Competitive Set
- **Positioning:** Affordable Mindbody alternative. ~$69/month entry. Aggressively targeting Mindbody refugees.
- **Weaknesses:** Built for yoga/spa/dance/salons — limited CrossFit/functional fitness depth · Hidden fees, contract auto-renewals, cancellation pain (documented Capterra) · Payment processing friction (Paragon) · Fees can change without notice
- **Win when:** Fitness-specific depth matters · SugarWOD wanted · Prospect burned by auto-renewal or hidden fees
- **Trap questions:** "When your CrossFit members ask what app to track PRs and WODs — what does WellnessLiving give you?" / "Have you priced out what WellnessLiving's payment processing actually costs per transaction?"
#### Gymdesk — Price-Sensitive Threat
- **Positioning:** All-features-included pricing model. Starts at ~$75/month with all features included at every tier — a deliberate counter to tiered platforms that lock features behind upgrades.
- **Strengths:** 4.8/5 rating on G2 and Capterra (high prospect-facing trust signal) · Transparent all-inclusive pricing · Gaining fast among price-sensitive martial arts and boutique studios who feel underserved by larger platforms · Active comparison content vs. Mindbody and PushPress
- **Weaknesses:** Limited brand recognition in CrossFit/functional fitness communities · No SugarWOD-equivalent · Less depth on Engage AI-style automation · Smaller customer base than ZP/PushPress/Mindbody
- **Win when:** CrossFit/functional fitness depth matters · Owner wants SugarWOD member experience · Wants Engage AI suite (Voice, Reviews, Workflow) · Multi-vertical operator
- **Lose when:** Cost-floor shopping is the only criterion · Prospect already in a Gymdesk trial and finds it sufficient · Owner values flat all-inclusive pricing over feature depth
- **Trap questions:** "When your members ask what app they should use to track WODs and PRs — what does Gymdesk give you?" / "How does Gymdesk handle automated review collection and missed-call answering — or do you do those manually?"

#### Glofox — Boutique-Brand Platform
- **Positioning:** Boutique fitness studio management. Targets group fitness, boutique studios, and chains. UK/Ireland origin, global presence.
- **Strengths:** Strong brand in boutique studio category · Branded member app · International presence (UK, EU, AU markets)
- **Weaknesses:** Built for boutique studio class-based model, not CrossFit/functional fitness workflows · No SugarWOD-equivalent · Less depth on functional fitness programming and performance tracking · Documented support and billing complaints
- **Win when:** Prospect needs CrossFit/functional fitness depth · Performance tracking and leaderboards matter · SugarWOD community engagement wanted · US-based with US-based support expectations
- **Lose when:** Prospect is a true boutique studio (barre, cycling, yoga-pilates hybrid) where Glofox's class-management depth is a closer fit · International prospect with regional Glofox support advantage
- **Trap question:** "When a CrossFit member wants to see their PR history and compare yesterday's WOD across the gym — what does Glofox show them?"

#### Vagaro — Wellness-Wide Platform
- **Positioning:** Wide wellness platform spanning salons, spas, fitness studios, and personal services. Strong in non-fitness verticals.
- **Strengths:** Large customer base in wellness · Marketplace presence (consumer app for booking) · Integrated payment processing · Multi-vertical scale
- **Weaknesses:** Same "built for salons and spas" problem as Mindbody — functional fitness is an afterthought · No SugarWOD-equivalent · No CrossFit-specific workflows · Marketplace economics similar to Mindbody (fees on consumer-app bookings)
- **Win when:** CrossFit/functional fitness specificity matters · Owner doesn't want to be on a salon-first platform · SugarWOD wanted · Owner has had bad experience with Mindbody-style marketplace fees
- **Lose when:** Multi-service operator (gym + spa + salon under one roof) where Vagaro's breadth wins · Prospect already relies on Vagaro marketplace for discovery
- **Trap question:** "How much of your monthly revenue goes back to Vagaro every time a member books through their consumer app — and what's your exit plan if those fees increase?"
