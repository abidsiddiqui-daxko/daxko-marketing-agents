## Martial Arts-Specific Features

### Belt Progression Tracking
The #1 differentiator for martial arts studios. Zen Planner tracks:
- Current belt/rank for every student
- Requirements for next promotion
- Testing eligibility and scheduling
- Promotion history and certificates
- Attendance requirements for belt testing

**Why it matters:** Belt progression IS the retention engine in martial arts. Students stay because
they're working toward their next rank. If your software doesn't track this natively, you're using
spreadsheets or paper — and students fall through the cracks.

### Family Memberships
Martial arts is a family activity. A typical studio has:
- Parent + 1-3 kids on the same account
- Different class schedules per family member
- Family discount pricing
- One billing relationship, multiple students

**Zen Planner handles this natively** — one account, multiple family members, different schedules,
family pricing. Competitors either can't do this or make it painful.

### Tournament Management
- Tournament registration and fee collection
- Weight class / division tracking
- Results recording
- Integration with tournament scheduling

## ClassPass Integration — Top-of-Funnel Strategy

ClassPass is a lead generation channel, not a competitor. Here's how to position it:

### The Play
1. ClassPass sends visitors to your intro classes
2. Visitors experience the community and instruction
3. Zen Planner's engagement tools (automated follow-ups, trial nurture campaigns) convert visitors to members
4. Campaign Marketplace has pre-built sequences for exactly this conversion path

### Talk Track
"ClassPass isn't your enemy — it's your top of funnel. Let ClassPass send you trial students.
Then let Zen Planner's automated follow-up campaigns turn those trials into long-term members.
We've got pre-built campaign sequences designed exactly for this — trial visit to first month
to long-term student. 2 clicks to set up."

### Why This Works for Martial Arts
- Martial arts has high trial-to-member potential (belt progression creates natural commitment)
- The community experience during a trial class is compelling
- Automated follow-ups catch the students who loved the class but forgot to sign up
- ClassPass visitors who convert become full-price members

## Messaging Do's and Don'ts (MA-Specific)

### Do
- Respect the discipline and tradition — these owners are proud instructors first
- Focus on student retention and enrollment growth
- Use discipline-specific language (students, not members; dojo, not gym)
- Highlight belt tracking and family billing as differentiators
- Reference the ClassPass opportunity as a growth channel
- Use peer proof from other martial arts studios specifically

### Don't
- Treat martial arts studios like generic gyms
- Use overly corporate language — keep it practical and personal
- Ignore the family dynamic — families are often 40-60% of revenue
- Dismiss belt progression as "just a feature" — it's the retention engine
- Compare martial arts to CrossFit or yoga — different culture entirely

### Competitive Updates — 2026

#### Wodify — Belt Tracking Confirmed Weakness
- **Belt tracking confirmed unusable for martial arts** — documented Trustpilot complaint: "belt tracking is literally impossible to use"
- **MA-specific win:** This is a confirmed, sourced differentiator — use in any Wodify competitive conversation involving MA studios
- **Wodify expanding into MA/yoga/jiu-jitsu** in 2026 — they are explicitly trying to enter the ZP segment. Counter with depth: belt progression, family billing, tournament management natively vs. Wodify bolted-on
- **Updated trap question:** "If you bring in 20 new leads this month — show me how Wodify converts them into long-term students with active belt progression tracked. What's automated, what's manual?"

#### Mindbody — Pricing Removal + ClassPass Ownership
- **Removed all pricing from website in 2026** — large active switcher pool opportunity among MA studios on Mindbody
- **20% marketplace fee** remains — still the strongest win angle: "That's $20 of every $100 intro class gone before the student walks in the door"
- **ClassPass acquired by Mindbody** — changes the positioning of Theme 4 (ClassPass-to-member conversion). ZP's ClassPass integration still exists independently, but some studios may feel locked into Mindbody if they rely heavily on ClassPass traffic
- **Updated trap questions:** "When you called support about belt tracking — did they know what you were talking about, or did you have to explain how a martial arts studio works?" / "If you turned off the Mindbody marketplace tomorrow, how would you replace those leads?"

#### WellnessLiving — Add to Competitive Set
- **Positioning:** Affordable Mindbody alternative. ~$69/month entry. Growing, but built for yoga/spa/dance/salons.
- **Weaknesses for MA:** Limited martial arts workflow depth · Belt tracking basic or absent · Hidden fees, contract auto-renewals (documented Capterra) · Payment processing friction · Fees can change without notice
- **Win when:** MA-specific depth matters (belt tracking, family billing, tournament) · Prospect burned by auto-renewal or hidden fees · SugarWOD equivalent wanted
- **Trap questions:** "Walk me through how WellnessLiving handles belt promotion eligibility tracking across 50 students." / "Have you priced out what WellnessLiving's payment processing actually costs per transaction?"

#### ZP Vulnerability — Address Proactively
- **ZP Capterra reviews** (bugs, slow system, registration issues) are visible to prospects researching alternatives
- **G2 shows PushPress rated better** on ongoing product support
- **Do not ignore this** — design "we heard you" messaging if these come up in discovery
- **Counter:** Dedicated 1:1 onboarding specialist, purpose-built for MA depth (belt tracking, family billing, tournament), Campaign Marketplace no competitor can replicate

#### Kicksite — MA-Vertical Point Solution
- **Positioning:** Dojo/academy management software focused narrowly on martial arts. Familiar name in MA communities.
- **Strengths:** Vertical familiarity (owners recognize the name) · Workflows designed for belt programs and student progression · Simple to use for single-discipline studios
- **Weaknesses vs. ZP:** Narrow platform — no Campaign Marketplace, no Engage AI suite, no SugarWOD-equivalent member-facing app, no integrated digital advertising · Limited reporting depth · No functional fitness coverage if studio adds a CrossFit or HIIT program later
- **Win when:** Multi-program studio (MA + camps + tournaments + retail) · Owner is growth-oriented and needs marketing automation, not just student management · Studio is expanding past single-discipline · Owner has been frustrated by Kicksite's lack of automated follow-up
- **Lose when:** Owner wants the absolute cheapest MA-specific tool · Single-discipline traditional dojo with no growth ambition · Strong existing trust with Kicksite from years of use
- **Trap question:** "When 20 new leads come in this month — show me how Kicksite turns them into long-term students with automated nurture, review requests, and missed-call recovery. What's automated, what's manual?"

#### Spark Membership — Low-Cost MA Entry
- **Positioning:** Low-cost membership management for martial arts and youth programs. Aggressive on price.
- **Strengths:** Low entry price · Simple workflows · Targets price-sensitive new studios
- **Weaknesses vs. ZP:** Same narrow platform problem as Kicksite — no Campaign Marketplace, no full marketing automation, no Engage AI, no SugarWOD-equivalent · Less established support model · Limited multi-location capabilities
- **Win when:** Owner has outgrown a price-floor tool and now needs scale infrastructure · Multi-location ambition · Wants the full Daxko platform (Flex Fees, Revenue Recovery, Engage AI)
- **Lose when:** Brand-new studio with <50 students prioritizing cost above all · Owner not yet ready for marketing automation
- **Trap question:** "When your billing fails, when a student misses three classes in a row, when a new lead comes in at 8pm — what does Spark do automatically, and what do you do manually?"
