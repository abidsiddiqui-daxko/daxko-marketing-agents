## Nonprofit Competitors

### TractionRec

**HQ:** Canada · **Pricing:** SaaS subscription + Salesforce licenses
**Target:** Parks & Rec, community centers, YMCAs, JCCs via Salesforce ecosystem

**Positioning:** CRM-based solution built on Salesforce. Promoting Agentforce AI as 24/7 registration agent. Growing YMCA/JCC adoption.

**Strengths:** Modern UI · Salesforce ecosystem · Agentforce AI positioning resonating with IT-forward YMCA/JCC leaders · Customization potential

**Weaknesses:** Requires ongoing Salesforce admin (permissions, workflow maintenance, release testing) · Steeper frontline UX · Salesforce licenses + add-ons expand TCO · Platform dependency on Salesforce release cycles

**Win when:**
- Prospect has limited IT resources and no Salesforce admin on staff
- Prospect is concerned about TCO (Salesforce licenses + TractionRec fees + admin costs)
- Prospect needs turnkey solution without heavy customization
- Front-line staff usability is a priority

**Lose when:**
- Prospect is deeply embedded in Salesforce ecosystem and sees unified CRM + ops as strategic
- Prospect has a dedicated Salesforce admin and values customization
- Prospect is attracted to Agentforce AI positioning
- Salesforce-first IT strategy is mandated

**Feature comparisons:**
- Daxko native mobile app vs. TractionRec no standard app (requires custom dev)
- Daxko built-in Engage vs. TractionRec requires Marketing Cloud/Pardot at extra cost
- Daxko vendor-managed, minimal IT burden vs. TractionRec customer-administered

**Trap question:** "Who on your team owns Salesforce admin, testing, and ongoing AI tuning — and what happens to your operations when that person leaves?"

**Win theme:** Purpose-built nonprofit operations vs. CRM-configured governance burden. Vendor-managed simplicity vs. customer-administered Salesforce admin.

**2026 watch:** Salesforce Agentforce AI positioning is resonating with tech-forward YMCA/JCC evaluators — AI feature parity perception risk. Counter: Daxko AI is native, not bolted on.

### Amilia

**HQ:** Montreal, Canada · **Pricing:** Transaction + subscription
**Target:** Recreation departments, municipalities, smaller nonprofits — growing JCC presence

**Positioning:** eCommerce/registration platform (Amilia SmartRec). "Responsible AI in recreation" thought leader. Targeting JCCs, Community Rec, and municipalities.

**Strengths:** Easy registration UX · Fast implementation · Cost-effective · AI thought leadership positioning

**Weaknesses:** No dedicated fundraising module · Billing errors and inaccurate reporting (documented user complaints) · Sales team overpromises features · Not a true all-in-one for larger orgs · Limited depth for large multi-site nonprofits

**Win when:**
- Prospect needs robust fundraising, donor management, and grant compliance — Amilia has none
- Prospect is a multi-site or complex nonprofit where Amilia's lightweight tools create friction
- Prospect has experienced Amilia billing errors or reporting inaccuracies (known user complaint)
- Prospect needs board-ready financial reporting

**Lose when:**
- Prospect is a smaller, simpler nonprofit prioritizing easy registration setup over operational depth
- Amilia's lower upfront cost and fast implementation win on budget
- Prospect perceives Daxko as too complex for their size
- Amilia's modern UX creates a stronger first impression

**Key messaging pillars vs. Amilia:**
1. Reporting & Financial Integrity
2. Engagement built-in, not bolted-on
3. Operational Strength for complex orgs
4. Delivered Innovation (vs. Amilia's AI roadmap promises)
5. One Member Identity

**Active counter-campaign:** daxko.com/considering-amilia (live)

**Migration proof points:** Rochester JCC, Siegel JCC

**Trap question:** "How do you handle donor management and grant compliance today — and when your finance team needs to reconcile transactions before a board meeting, how many systems do they touch?"

**Win theme:** Real-time accurate data vs. reporting gaps. Built-in fundraising vs. none. Risk management built-in vs. none. Scalability for multi-site vs. best suited for smaller orgs.

## Club Competitors

### ABC Fitness

**HQ:** US-based · **Pricing:** Enterprise — full-service payfac model, not publicly listed
**Target:** Large franchise operators and national chains (e.g. Planet Fitness)

**Strengths:**
- Full-service payfac with next-day ACH funding and revenue recovery tools
- Strong franchise and multi-location management (member reciprocity, centralized payment routing)
- High brand recognition among national chains
- Ability to pass transaction fees to customers

**Weaknesses:**
- Rigid — 7–10 day delay for basic membership changes
- Slow customer support for time-sensitive updates
- No dynamic reporting — fragmented data, no accrual accounting
- Expensive, restrictive API access
- Mobile app lacks native core system integration — delayed sync

**Win when:** Clubs want control over billing, refunds, and fund timing. Operators frustrated with delayed changes, limited financial visibility, or fragmented billing.

**Lose when:** Large franchise environments already standardized on ABC's payfac model. Hands-off billing + brand familiarity outweigh need for flexibility.

**Trap question:** "How much control do you actually have today over your billing, refunds, and when your funds hit your account?"

**Win themes:**
- Self-service flexibility vs. support dependency
- Speed of change vs. 7–10 day delays
- Real-time flexible reporting with accrual accounting vs. fragmented data
- Open API at competitive rates vs. expensive restrictions
- Unified mobile experience with real-time sync

### Mindbody (Club Segment)

**HQ:** San Luis Obispo, CA · **Pricing:** Tiered SaaS + add-ons
**Target:** Boutique and class-based studios primarily (yoga, Pilates, spin). Attempting larger gyms but struggles with complex club operations. ~60,000 businesses globally.

**Strengths:** Large brand name and consumer marketplace · ClassPass partnership · AI tools in premium tiers · Wide integration marketplace · Members recognize Mindbody consumer app

**Weaknesses:**
- Studio/spa-first architecture — lacks club-specific depth
- Manual workarounds for family/group memberships
- Add-ons (BrandBot, marketing tools) inflate total cost significantly
- Consumer marketplace charges 20% fee per new client
- Reporting not optimized for complex club operations
- No native access control — requires third-party systems

**Win when:** Clubs outgrow basic class scheduling — multi-amenity facilities, family memberships, multi-location reporting expose Mindbody's limitations.

**Lose when:** Boutique/studio-focused business prioritizing simplicity, price, or marketplace exposure.

**Trap question:** "How are you currently managing family memberships or non-class offerings like courts, childcare, or programs?"

**Feature comparisons:**
- CA: built for complex memberships (family, corporate, multi-tier, access restrictions) · Mindbody: basic packages, limited family/group support
- CA: advanced class, courts, swim lanes, childcare — all in one · Mindbody: strong for appointment-style services
- CA: integrated 24/7 access with key fob/barcode control · Mindbody: no native access control

### Zenoti

**HQ:** Seattle, WA (founded 2010) · **Pricing:** Enterprise — opaque, add-ons required. $1B unicorn valuation 2020.
**Target:** Large multi-location chains — spas, salons, now entering fitness/wellness. ~30,000 businesses globally.

**Strengths:**
- Modern, sleek UI
- Native AI assistant ('Zeenie'), churn prediction, phone bot, automated campaigns
- Advanced in-platform marketing tools
- Multi-location and franchise support
- Heavily VC-backed with active product development

**Weaknesses:**
- Spa-first architecture — lacks club-specific depth (no true facility scheduling, no batch EFT, limited household/family grouping)
- Steep learning curve and setup complexity
- Mixed support reviews — global team, inconsistent fitness experience
- Known UI/UX glitches and reliability issues
- Opaque pricing — add-ons inflate TCO

**Win when:** Clubs need system built specifically for their operations — multi-purpose facilities, complex memberships, teams valuing usability and support.

**Lose when:** Buyers drawn to modern UI or AI features early, or when spa-style operations are core to the business.

**Trap question:** "How do you currently manage family memberships or facility reservations like courts and swim lanes?"

**Red flags — when Zenoti resonates:** Prospect heavily emphasizes spa/salon operations · Enamored with Zenoti's UI or AI early · Requires features outside typical club scope.

### Jonas Fitness

**HQ:** US-based · **Pricing:** Traditional — includes optional outsourced Member Billing Services
**Target:** YMCAs, hospital-based wellness centers, medical fitness facilities. Long industry tenure.

**Strengths:**
- Long industry tenure — trusted by YMCAs, hospital wellness, medical fitness
- Strong billing + optional outsourced collections service
- Robust check-in showing member photo, notes, and account info on one screen
- Childcare check-in shows time in studio and parent phone numbers
- Flexible childcare bookings (1-hr and 2-hr in same day)
- Large integration ecosystem (e.g. Welld Health for physician referrals and insurance)

**Weaknesses:**
- Frequent downtime, crashes, and slowness
- Complex multi-step workflows for scheduling, event setup, agreement rewrites
- Manual billing tasks — pro-rating freezes, double card entry, confusing corporate accounts
- No native mobile app (relies on 3rd-party vendors like MiGym)
- Reporting difficult and inconsistent — requires ad hoc queries
- Long support hold times
- System architecture dated and fragmented

**Win when:** Clubs want a modern, flexible system that reduces manual work. Operators frustrated with legacy workflows, limited usability, and the need for workarounds.

**Lose when:** Clubs deeply entrenched in Jonas and hesitant to switch. Most common in orgs prioritizing familiarity over modernization, or where switching costs feel prohibitive.

**Trap question:** "How much manual work or workarounds does your team still rely on day-to-day to run operations?"

**CA honest limitations vs. Jonas:** Newer in medical fitness — less historical footprint · Does not provide outsourced billing/collections service · Stricter childcare booking rules · Jonas check-in screens show richer at-a-glance info.

**Win themes:** CA cloud reliability (99.9998% delivered uptime vs. Jonas downtime) · Automated billing · Native mobile · Modern UX.
