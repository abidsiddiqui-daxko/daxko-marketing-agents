## Same-Store Sales (SSS) Net Revenue Model

### What SSS Means for Club Buyers

Same-store sales net revenue is the north star metric for commercial fitness operators. It measures revenue growth at existing locations (excluding new openings), revealing the true health of the business. Club operators live and die by this number because it strips out expansion noise and shows whether each location is actually growing.

**2026 OKR Target:** Positive YoY SSS Net Revenue for 70% of Daxko club customers.

Daxko's platform contributes to SSS improvement through three primary levers:
1. **Revenue recovery** — Reducing payment failures and reclaiming lost revenue
2. **Revenue expansion** — Increasing revenue per member through better billing, upsells, and program utilization
3. **Revenue protection** — Improving retention to reduce churn-driven revenue loss

### Revenue Components

| Component | Description | Daxko Impact |
|-----------|------------|--------------|
| **Membership dues** | Monthly/annual member payments — the core revenue stream | Automated billing, dunning, card updater services reduce involuntary churn. Online self-service joins target: 40% |
| **Ancillary revenue** | Personal training, retail, F&B, spa services | Program management, POS integration, upsell visibility through reporting |
| **Payment processing** | Collection rates, failed payment recovery, transaction fees | Integrated in-house processing via Daxko Payment Services. Flex Fees pass transaction costs to members |
| **Program fees** | Classes, camps, special programs, day passes | Scheduling, online booking, capacity management |

### Key Metrics Club Buyers Track

| Metric | Why It Matters | Daxko Lever |
|--------|---------------|-------------|
| SSS net revenue growth (% YoY) | Overall business health | All platform capabilities compound here |
| Revenue per square foot | Location efficiency | Reporting visibility drives optimization decisions |
| Revenue per member per month | Monetization effectiveness | Upsell automation, program utilization tracking |
| Payment collection rate | Revenue leakage prevention | Card approval rate improvement target: +5% |

| Churn rate (monthly/annual) | Revenue protection | Automated engagement, at-risk member identification |

## Payments Positioning

### Daxko Payment Services
Daxko's payment processing is integrated and in-house — not a third-party bolt-on. This means:
- **Single vendor** for software and payments — no finger-pointing between providers
- **Integrated reporting** — payment data flows directly into member and financial reports
- **Card approval rate improvement** — 2026 target is +5% improvement across the customer base
- **Automated collections** — retry logic, card updater services, and dunning sequences reduce involuntary churn

### Flex Fees
Flex Fees are a revenue driver that passes transaction processing costs to members, preserving the club's margin on every transaction. This is increasingly standard in the industry and positions clubs to:
- Eliminate payment processing as a cost center
- Maintain or improve net revenue per member
- Stay competitive with operators already using surcharging

**Proof point:** College Park — +3% bottom-line improvement after implementing Flex Fees.

### Common Objections About Switching Payment Processors
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Switching is risky" | Daxko handles migration with dedicated payments team. No gap in processing |
| "We'll lose next-day funding" | Daxko offers competitive funding timelines with integrated reporting advantage |
| "Members will push back on Flex Fees" | Industry adoption is accelerating. Most members don't notice or accept it. College Park saw no material pushback |
| "Our rates are already competitive" | It's not just rates — it's recovery. +5% card approval reclaims revenue you're currently losing |

## Implementation Velocity

**2026 Target:** Reduce implementation configuration time by 10-25%

### Why Implementation Speed Matters
Club operators measure switching cost in two currencies: dollars and downtime. Every week of implementation is a week of:
- Staff split between old and new systems
- Risk of billing errors during migration
- Member experience disruption
- Delayed ROI on the new platform

### Daxko Implementation Approach
- Dedicated implementation team with club-specific expertise
- Phased rollout reduces risk for multi-location operators
- Payment migration handled by specialized payments team
- Training delivered by people who understand club operations, not generic software trainers
