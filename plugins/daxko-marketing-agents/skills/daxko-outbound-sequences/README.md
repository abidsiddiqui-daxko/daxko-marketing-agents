# Daxko Outbound Sequences — Agent 19 — BDR/AM Sequence Builder

**Version:** 1.10.1 · **Skill name:** `daxko-outbound-sequences` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui

## What it does (in plain words)
Writes outreach that Daxko's reps send themselves: one cold email, or a sequence of steps over a few weeks.
You choose the channels — **email, calls, LinkedIn, SMS or social DMs, on their own or mixed** — and it gives back only those.
It also writes follow-up for people who downloaded a report or guide, built around the findings you paste, up to 12+ touches.
It works for BDRs and SDRs writing to new organizations, and for account managers writing to existing customers.

## How to use it
1. Type a simple ask, for example:
   - "Cold email to a YMCA executive director"
   - "BDR sequence for JCCs"
   - "SDR cadence for multi-location clubs, calls and LinkedIn only"
   - "Outreach to our Club Automation customers about Payments"
   - "Fix this prospecting sequence" (paste it)
2. Answer its short questions (at most 3 at a time): segment, buyer, new or existing contact, goal, product,
   channels, number of touches. Say "just write it" and it uses sensible defaults and tells you which.
3. You get: an at-a-glance table, every step written out, the fields to fill (like `{First Name}`) and a
   checklist before it goes live.

## What it will never do
- Invent numbers, customers, quotes, links or prices, or say a roadmap AI feature is available.
- Use urgency ("last chance") or sales words with nonprofit buyers.
- Attack a competitor in copy the prospect reads.
- Keep or repeat names, emails or phone numbers from a contact list.
- Send, schedule, enroll anyone or load anything into a sales tool. It writes drafts; a person sends.

## Before anything goes out
Run the draft through Agent 9 — Brand Guardian (`daxko-brand-guardian`), then the rep reviews and sends.
