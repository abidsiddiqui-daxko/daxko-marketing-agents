## How to pick proof — match it to the product

Case studies are product-specific. Always know which product or service the outreach is about,
because that decides which proof is usable.

| Outreach is about | Use |
|---|---|
| A named product | Proof that features **that** product, plus company-level scale numbers |
| Not product-specific | Company-level proof only (scale numbers). **No product case study** |

Never stretch a case study to a product it doesn't feature. A JCC story about Daxko Operations is
not evidence for Daxko Payments.

products, but the list has not been supplied). Until it is: for YMCA outreach use company-level
proof and the Y-USA alliance reference, and say plainly that no cleared YMCA case study is
available — never substitute a JCC one.

## Buying committees, by nonprofit sub-segment

| Sub-segment | Primary decision makers | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| YMCA | CEO / Executive Director · COO / VP Operations | Consensus-driven and multi-layered. Major spend needs CEO sponsorship and board approval. Peer influence is significant — Ys consult Y-USA, regional networks and neighbouring Ys |
| JCC | Executive Director · Operations Director | Finance Director and board matter for capital investment. Accumulated point solutions cause real administrative friction |
| Boys & Girls Clubs | Executive Director / CEO | Centralised and lean — the ED is the authority. Board scrutinises ROI and mission alignment. Attendance, enrolment and outcomes reporting for funders is the recurring pain |
| Community centers | Executive Director / Center Director · Parks & Recreation Director | Municipal ones bring city IT and procurement. Often replacing outdated desktop software, paper forms or spreadsheets. Must report to funders, boards or city councils |

"Nonprofit" on its own is not a segment — always ask which kind.

## Trigger signals worth referencing

Real reasons for reaching out now, used instead of manufactured urgency: new CEO, COO or CIO
appointment · facility expansion, merger or capital campaign · an RFP or job posting mentioning
system integrations · a posting for "Data Manager", "Outcomes Coordinator" or "Development Manager" ·
a known Salesforce or TractionRec evaluation · budget or board cycle timing.

## Cadence — default shapes (general practice from demand gen, not a Daxko rule)

Day numbers are calendar days from enrolment and are weekday-safe **only for a Monday start**.

| Touches / span | Day numbers | Lands on (Monday start) |
|---|---|---|
| 4 over 2 weeks | 1 · 3 · 8 · 12 | Mon Wed Mon Fri |
| 6 over 3 weeks | 1 · 2 · 5 · 9 · 15 · 19 | Mon Tue Fri Tue Mon Fri |
| 8 over 4 weeks | 1 · 2 · 4 · 8 · 12 · 17 · 22 · 26 | Mon Tue Thu Mon Fri Wed Mon Fri |
| 10 over 6 weeks | 1 · 2 · 4 · 8 · 12 · 17 · 23 · 29 · 36 · 40 | Mon Tue Thu Mon Fri Wed Tue Mon Mon Fri |

Ask which day enrolment starts, or say Monday was assumed. A step that lands on a Saturday or
Sunday moves to the next business day; print the day numbers actually used. Front-load, then
spread out.

## Step craft

- **Emails default to manual** (the rep reviews and sends). Automated only when asked.
- **An automated email carries no per-contact blank** — nothing reviews it, so a blank ships to the
  prospect exactly as typed. Either make the step manual or use a field the sales tool fills.
  Every automated email states its trigger, that send-from maps to the rep, and a test send before
  go-live.
- Never two automated emails on consecutive days.
- One subject line per email (never two options, even if asked — offer the other angle on request),
  a preview line, sentence case, one call to action.
- Follow-up emails reply in thread and reuse the subject; a new angle gets a new subject.
- **Call step:** objective · opener as spoken · if they engage, 2–4 discovery questions then the
  ask — get a specific day and time and confirm it out loud, never "I'll send you a link" ·
  if not, a graceful pivot · 2–3 likely objections with replies · voicemail script (short, one
  reason to call back). Voicemail lives inside the call step and does not count as a touch.
- **LinkedIn connection note:** 300 characters or fewer including spaces and filled-in fields;
  no links; no subject. **LinkedIn message:** a short paragraph or two, one idea, one question.
  A connection request comes before any LinkedIn message.
- A step that mentions a physical item gets a task before it confirming the item arrived.
- The last touch closes the loop honestly — "I don't want to be another thing in your inbox, so this
  is my last note for a while" — it never threatens.
- Open with an at-a-glance table built from the steps, then check every row against its step —
  same count, same channels, same days.

## Voice and words for outreach (core files win)

- Nonprofit: practical and experienced, confident but measured. Club: commercially direct.
  Boutique: warm and personal.
- If a sentence would sound at home in a venture-backed SaaS pitch, it does not belong. If AI sounds
  like a feature to sell instead of work getting done, it is off-voice.
- **Urgency is a category ban, not a word list** — "last chance", "act now", "closing out my
  quarter", "last attempt before I close your file". An outright ban in Nonprofit. A real stated
  deadline is a fact, not urgency. Use a real reason for the timing (a board cycle, a season) or
  simply say you'll stop.
- Toward nonprofit buyers never: revenue, sell, churn rate, CLV, conversion rate, or "close" in its
  sales sense. Ordinary English ("closing the loop") is fine — judge the meaning.
- House call to action for a meeting: *"See it in action — book a 20-minute demo."* "Learn more",
  "click here" and "contact us" are banned; name the thing. A button label is Title Case.
- Full product name on first use ("Daxko Payments", "Club Automation"). There is no product called
  "Daxko Core".
- **AI claims follow `product-knowledge.md` `## AI Capabilities — BY DELIVERY STATUS` only.** The
  "live now / still coming" list in the original guidance is out of date and conflicts with the core
  file (it lists dynamic segmentation as live; the core file dates it Early 2027) — **it is not used.**
