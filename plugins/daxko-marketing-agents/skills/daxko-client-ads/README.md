# Agent 36 — Paid Digital — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-client-ads` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.10.0

## What it does
Builds ONE new paid ad campaign for a Digital Services client (Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn) as a campaign build sheet: structure, targeting, budget and dates as you give them, keywords as you paste them, ad text options, names, UTM tags and what conversions to track. It asks you short rounds of questions (up to 3 at a time) until it has what the job needs, and shows a checklist of what is given and what is still missing.

## Requests to try
- "Build the Google Ads campaign for our YMCA client's spring membership drive"
- "Set up a Meta campaign for this club client's summer camp"
- "Campaign build sheet for the JCC client's open house ads"

## What it may change, and only after your yes
If you have a connector to the client's ad account, it can create the new campaign, ad groups and ads **paused** (off — they spend nothing). It first shows you the exact list and waits for your "yes" in the chat. You become the owner of that change.

## What it never does
Turn an ad on, change a live campaign, budget or bid, delete anything, or spend money. A person on the Digital Services team does that after Agent 73 — Pre-Launch QA says go and the client signs off.
