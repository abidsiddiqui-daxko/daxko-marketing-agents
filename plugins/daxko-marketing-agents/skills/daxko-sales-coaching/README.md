# Agent 57 — Sales Coaching — teammate guide

**Skill:** `daxko-sales-coaching` · **Owner:** Abid Siddiqui · **Version:** 1.7.33

## What it does
Coaches **one sales rep on one new-business call at a time**, from Daxko's own files. It can play a practice buyer, help you get ready for one call, or review one call you paste — and tells you what worked, what missed, and the approved line to use instead, with the file it comes from.

## Requests to try
- "Practise a discovery call with me — you're the CFO of a mid-size YMCA" (say **stop** or **how did I do?** to get feedback)
- "Role-play 'Amilia is cheaper' with me — you're a JCC executive director"
- "Prep me for my call tomorrow with a JCC executive director"
- "How did I do on this call?" (paste one transcript or your notes — remove names, emails and phone numbers)

## What you get
A practice buyer built only from real pains, objections and trap questions on file (always labelled "practice scenario — not a real customer"), or a call plan, or a call review. Then a feedback table (what you said · worked or missed · the rule and file · what to say instead), 1–3 coaching points, one drill, approved lines in two columns (internal fact | what you may say), a NOT ON FILE list, the Key Result it serves, and sources with dates.

## Good to know
- **No scores.** There is no scoring rubric on file, so it never gives a number or ranks reps.
- One call at a time. It refuses bulk Gong, call or CRM exports, and never repeats names, emails or phones.
- It never teaches a price, a free trial, a competitor attack or roadmap AI (Member Intelligence 360°, AI Agents, Intelligent Scoring) — not even as "coming".
- MEDDIC or any named sales method, talk-ratio benchmarks and per-rep win rates are not on file.
- It records, sends and updates nothing. Who signs off coaching material is not on file.
